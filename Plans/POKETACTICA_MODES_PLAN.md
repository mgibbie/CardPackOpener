# Poketactica: a plan for every mode

Owner, 2026-11-25: "Make a plan to improve each of the Poketactica modes. No daily or weekly challenges."

This plan **replaces** the four improvement plans of 2026-11-24 (`POKETACTICA_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md` and the Item World,
Maze Builder and Mystery Maze ones). What they still had unbuilt is carried here where it is still worth doing, marked
*(carried)*. Their "Built" sections stay as the record of what shipped.

## The owner's answers (2026-11-25)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which modes get the most | **All of them**: Story and Dungeon, Mystery Maze, Tower Defense and Maze Builder, Item World and Versus |
| What kind of improvement | **All four**: feel and polish, more to do, deeper choices, reasons to come back |
| The old plans' leftovers | **Fold them in** |
| Difficulty | **About right, keep it.** New things land at today's difficulty |
| The one thing to put first | **Screens are hard to read** |
| Standing | **No daily or weekly challenges** (and, from before: no daily runs or dungeons, no weekly tournament) |

Sizes: S a day or less, M a few days, L a week or more.

## Order
1. **A1 to A7** (screens and feel), one mode at a time: Mystery Maze, then battles, then Tower Defense, then the camp.
2. One round of each mode's small items: **M8, M9, M5, T2, T3, B5, B6, I1, I3, D4, D5, V1 to V3, V5, K2**.
3. The medium items a mode at a time, most-played first: **M1 to M4, M7**, then **B1 to B4**, then **S3, S4, D1, D2, D3**, **T1, T4**, **I2, I4**, **V4**, **R1 to R3**.
4. The large ones last: **S1, S2, M6, K1**.

## Rules this plan keeps
- A fallen figurine is gone for good; no buy-back.
- A change to a run's rules bumps that mode's rules revision, and a run or replay recorded before it plays as it was.
- Every balance change is measured by the auto-players before and after, and the numbers are written here.
- Keyboard, controller and touch all keep working; no Poketactica item shares a main-game item's name.

## How to tell it worked
- A new player can say what each camp screen is for after one look, on a phone.
- Every mode has at least one thing to aim at after its first clear that is not "do it again".
- The auto-players' clear rates stay within a few points of today's.

## Built (2026-11-27): small items, moves to teach inside a run (M5; rules 22)

- A **Lesson Slate** lies on about 15 floors in 100 of a Mystery Maze. Read by the leader, or handed to a partner, it
  teaches the next move the reader's kind would learn, for the rest of that run. With four moves, one makes way: the
  bag asks which. One that has passed every move cannot read it, and the slate is kept.
- The move is for the run only: the figurine's own moves at the camp are unchanged.
- It is dealt by dice of its own, so every other roll of a run falls as it did; a run recorded under rules 21 finds none.
- Not measured: 15 in 100 is a first number. Shops do not sell slates, and the auto-players do not read them.

## Built (2026-11-27): small items, the plain held items gain by level (I1; battle rules 13)

- Ten held items had no number of their own, so a level gave them slots and nothing else. Each now gains from its
  level: nothing at level 1, about 10% at level 50, 20% at level 100.
  - The seven curing beads and White Moss also **heal** that much of max HP when they work.
  - Focus Thread **leaves** that much of max HP (1 HP at level 1). The forecast says so.
  - Scope Sight's critical hits **deal** that much more.
- A level 1 item plays as before. Battles recorded under rules 12, Tower Defense runs under 18 and Mystery Maze runs
  under 22 play levelled plain items as they did (Tower Defense is now rules 19, Mystery Maze 23).
- Not measured: 20% at level 100 is a first number, chosen to sit under the other items' tripled bonuses.

## Built (2026-11-27): small items, Training says what it is for (D4)

- Training asks **what to practise** before the team is picked:
  - **A free battle**: the wild four at your level, the battle Training always was.
  - **Combos**: three sturdy foes that stand still.
  - **Throwing**: foes on the ledges, two and three levels up.
  - **Catching**: wild Pokémon by your base, starting on a fifth of their HP.
  - **Type matchups**: a foe for each of your first four, each weak to that one (by the game's own type chart).
- Each practice says what to try as the battle opens, and again from the field menu ("What to practise").
- Same ground, same pay (half), nobody lost. No rules revision: a foe may now start hurt, which only these setups use.

## Built (2026-11-27): small items, the Dungeon's records (D5)

- The Dungeon kept one number, the deepest boss beaten. Three more are kept and shown on its screen:
  - **Deepest floor cleared**, boss or not.
  - **Most floors in a row with nobody lost**, in one run (with "this run: n" while a run has a streak).
  - **Fastest boss**: the fewest turns a boss floor was won in.
- A cleared floor's results say "New record!" and which it set.
- They are synced with the collection; between two devices the better one wins.
- Not built: the plan's "fewest lost" as a count over a whole run. The no-loss streak stands in for it.

## Built (2026-11-27): small items, a check before sharing a map (K2)

- "Share code" in the Map maker checks the map first and says what is wrong in words, by base number: a base on a
  gap, bases that cannot reach each other on foot, fewer than two bases, a size out of range.
- A map that cannot be played is not shared, and the server refuses it too, in the same words.
- A surprise is a warning, not a stop: a wild Pokémon next to a base, on a gap, or walled in where no base can walk
  to it. The map can be shared "anyway" once it has been said.
- Not changed: maps already shared keep their codes, broken or not.

## Built (2026-11-27): small items, a turn clock both players can see (V5)

- Optional and off by default: the host picks off, 60 s, 90 s or 2 minutes when making the match.
- A phase lasts that long in all (an action does not start it again); the server ends it when it runs out.
- Every player's screen counts it down in the top bar, from the server's time, red for the last ten seconds. The lobby
  says which clock the match has.
- With the clock off a match plays as before: a side that does nothing for two and a half minutes has its phase ended.

## Built (2026-11-27): small items, a record with each player and a rematch (V2)

- A Versus match's results say your wins and losses against each other player ("You and bob: 3 wins, 1 loss"); the
  lobby shows "you 3–1" beside a player you have met. Computers are in nobody's record.
- **Rematch** (Z) on the results makes a new lobby with the same rules (the turn clock too), players and teams. The first
  to ask hosts it; anyone else pressing Rematch is put in the same one, and is told one is waiting.
- The record is one kept row a player (`ptrec:<user>`, the last 50 met), written once when a match's end is noted, from
  the result the pages report: a friendly record, as trusted as the lobby's own note of the winner.
- With this the round of small items in the plan's order (step 2) is done.

## Built (2026-11-27): medium items, challenge rules you choose before a run (M1; Mystery Maze rules 24)

- On the party screen of a dungeon that can be cleared, three rules can be switched on before going in (never daily):
  - **No items**: the bag starts empty, and nothing but stars and keys is picked up or bought. There is no pack screen.
  - **Alone**: one figurine goes in, and nobody joins on the way.
  - **Timed**: each floor must be left within 250 turns, or the run is over. The bar counts them down and warns at
    100, 50, 20 and 10.
- Each makes the clear prize half as big again (all three: 2.5 times), shown before entering ("clearing pays 375 ★").
- Each is marked on the dungeon, for good, once cleared with it; the marks sync, and one earned on either device counts.
- Not for The Abyss (it has no end), the Lv 5 dungeons or Item World dives.
- A run recorded under rules 23 has none. A run's recipe carries its rules, so resume, spectating and replays agree.
- Not measured: 250 turns and +50% each are first numbers.

## Built (2026-11-27): medium items, milestones in The Abyss (M2)

- Reaching floors 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100 of The Abyss each opens something to keep: one more item in the **kit** every
  Abyss run then starts with, free (an Oran Bead, an Escape Marble, a Max Joint Oil, a Wax Banquet, a Sitrus Bead).
- A milestone counts when the floor is REACHED, whether or not the run comes back. The results say "Milestone!".
