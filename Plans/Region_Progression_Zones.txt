 MAGEPUNK / CardPackOpener  —  PROGRESSION BY ZONE  (CROSS-REGION INTERLEAVE)
 The main quest is now ONE interleaved circuit across all three regions, gated by
 authentic per-spot obstacles + inter-region PORTALS.
================================================================================
 Generated from the live code (overworld/quest.js + blockers.js + portals.js +
 badges.js + flydata.js), commit 27fbdd94, 2026-08-17.

 WHAT CHANGED THIS REVISION  (badge-thirds + portals)
   The three regions used to be independent, isolated campaigns. They are now a
   single interleaved circuit:

     Each gym gives a THIRD of a tier's badge. You must beat gym N in ALL THREE
     regions (Kanto + Johto + Hoenn) before you can advance to gym N+1 in ANY of
     them. JohKanto (the Gen-2 Kanto 16-badge post-game path) is NOT part of this.

   Because the three regions are otherwise separate maps you can't walk between,
   every gym town now has an INTER-REGION PORTAL pad beside its Pokemon Center that
   links the three regions' SAME-tier gym towns. Step onto it (face it, press A) and
   pick a destination — it flies you to that region's same-tier gym town and makes
   it your current region. The portals are what make beating gym N in all three
   regions possible in one save.

 THE GLOBAL TIER RULE  (how "zones" work now)
   TIER T = gym T cleared in EVERY region (T badges in Kanto AND Johto AND Hoenn).
   Within tier T, each region's gym T+1 is the current challenge. You can beat your
   region's gym T+1 (earning its badge) — but that region then WAITS: its road into
   the gym-(T+2) zone stays sealed until the OTHER two regions also clear gym T+1.
   A region can be at most ONE gym ahead of the others; then it has to help the
   laggards catch up (portal over and beat their gym) before anyone advances.
   The objective line tells you exactly who's behind, e.g.:
     "KANTO is ahead! Beat GYM 1 in the other regions — FALKNER in VIOLET CITY and
      ROXANNE in RUSTBORO CITY — to advance. Take the PORTAL by the POKeMON CENTER."

 ZONE = TIER
   Zone N below = the area that opens in a region once TIER N is reached (gym N
   cleared in all three regions). Zone 0 = before any gym. The gym inside zone N is
   the (N+1)th; clearing it in all three regions opens zone N+1 everywhere. Zone 8 =
   the Pokemon League (opens once every region has all 8). "DONE" = Champion.

 THE OBSTACLE TYPES  (all still SEEN and walked-into — never an invisible check)
   GUARD   — a gym guide / officer who steps aside once the tier is cleared
             EVERYWHERE (it now checks the shared tier, not just this region).
   VILLAIN — TEAM ROCKET/AQUA occupying a spot until their boss is beaten (this is
             a per-region story beat — local, not cross-region).
   MON     — a Pokemon (Snorlax / Sudowoodo / Kecleon) cleared by a KEY ITEM (local).
   TERRAIN — water / dive / a cut tree that an HM opens (HM availability stays
             per-region — an ability you earned, not a tier gate).
   PORTAL  — the pad by each gym town's Pokemon Center; links the three regions'
             same-tier gym towns and flips your current region on use.

 PRECONDITION: pick a region and receive a starter from its lab. You begin in that
   region's start town; the portal in each gym town opens the rest of the journey.

#  KANTO   (start PALLET TOWN)
################################################################################
 Gyms: Brock·Boulder, Misty·Cascade, Surge·Thunder, Erika·Rainbow, Koga·Soul,
       Sabrina·Marsh, Blaine·Volcano, Giovanni·Earth.
 Key items: POKe FLUTE (from MR. FUJI in LAVENDER, after the CELADON Rocket
   Hideout is cleared) — wakes the two SNORLAX.

ZONE 0  (tier 0) — Pallet, Route 1-2, Viridian Forest, PEWTER + BROCK.
  PORTAL @ PEWTER → VIOLET CITY (Johto) · RUSTBORO CITY (Hoenn).
  >> To ZONE 1: a GYM GUIDE blocks the east road out of Pewter (Route 3):
     "Come back with the BOULDER BADGE!" — steps aside once gym 1 is beaten in ALL
     THREE regions (Brock + Falkner + Roxanne). Portal over to beat the others.

ZONE 1  (tier 1) — Route 3, Mt. Moon, Route 4, CERULEAN + MISTY, Nugget Bridge.
  PORTAL @ CERULEAN → AZALEA TOWN (Johto) · DEWFORD TOWN (Hoenn).
  >> To ZONE 2: a GYM GUIDE at the Vermilion approach (Route 6) wants the tier-2
     badge — beat MISTY here AND Bugsy + Brawly. (The eastern back-route past
     Lavender is sealed by the two SNORLAX below.)

ZONE 2  (tier 2) — VERMILION + LT. SURGE, S.S. Anne, Diglett's Cave.
  PORTAL @ VERMILION → GOLDENROD CITY (Johto) · MAUVILLE CITY (Hoenn).
  >> To ZONE 3: a GYM GUIDE at the Celadon approach (Route 7) wants tier 3 — beat
     LT. SURGE + Whitney + Wattson.

ZONE 3  (tier 3) — CELADON + ERIKA, Game Corner + ROCKET HIDEOUT.
  PORTAL @ CELADON → ECRUTEAK CITY (Johto) · LAVARIDGE TOWN (Hoenn).
  KEY EVENT (local): beating GIOVANNI at the Rocket Hideout lets MR. FUJI (Lavender)
     give you the POKe FLUTE — which wakes the two road-blocking SNORLAX:
       • SNORLAX on ROUTE 12  and  • SNORLAX on ROUTE 16.
  >> To ZONE 4: a GYM GUIDE at the Fuchsia approach (Route 18) wants tier 4 — beat
     ERIKA + Morty + Flannery.

ZONE 4  (tier 4) — FUCHSIA + KOGA, Safari Zone, Cycling Road.
  PORTAL @ FUCHSIA → CIANWOOD CITY (Johto) · PETALBURG CITY (Hoenn).
  >> To ZONE 5: TEAM ROCKET occupies SAFFRON — a ROCKET GRUNT bars SABRINA's gym
     ("the GYM's closed — boss's orders"). Clear SILPH CO. to drive them out (local
     story). Tier advances when SABRINA + Chuck + Norman are all beaten.

ZONE 5  (tier 5) — SAFFRON + SILPH CO. + SABRINA (once Rocket is gone).
  PORTAL @ SAFFRON → OLIVINE CITY (Johto) · FORTREE CITY (Hoenn).
  >> To ZONE 6: CINNABAR is across the sea — you need SURF (a Kanto ability, usable
     at 5 Kanto badges). On the island a GYM GUIDE bars BLAINE's gym until tier 6 is
     cleared — beat BLAINE + Jasmine + Winona.

ZONE 6  (tier 6) — CINNABAR + BLAINE, Pokemon Mansion.
  PORTAL @ CINNABAR → MAHOGANY TOWN (Johto) · MOSSDEEP CITY (Hoenn).
  >> To ZONE 7: a GYM GUIDE at VIRIDIAN's long-shut gym says only a top-tier trainer
     can draw the LEADER out — beat BLAINE + Pryce + Tate&Liza (tier 7).

ZONE 7  (tier 7) — VIRIDIAN GYM + GIOVANNI.
  PORTAL @ VIRIDIAN → BLACKTHORN CITY (Johto) · SOOTOPOLIS CITY (Hoenn).
  >> To ZONE 8: an OFFICER on the Route 22 gate checks all 8 badges before the
     POKeMON LEAGUE. Beat GIOVANNI + Clair + Wallace (tier 8) — then every League
     opens together.

ZONE 8  (LEAGUE) — Victory Road, Indigo Plateau, Elite Four, CHAMPION (Blue).

 QUICK REFERENCE — the boundary obstacle in each region  (now checks the SHARED tier)
================================================================================
 The per-spot obstacle is unchanged; its condition is now the GLOBAL tier (gym N
 cleared in all three regions), and each gym town has a PORTAL to the same-tier towns.

 KANTO:  0→1 Pewter/Route3 GUIDE | 1→2 Vermilion GUIDE | 2→3 Celadon GUIDE | 3→4
         Fuchsia GUIDE (+ Snorlax×2 need the POKe FLUTE, local) | 4→5 Saffron ROCKET
         (beat Silph, local) | 5→6 SURF + Cinnabar GUIDE | 6→7 Viridian GUIDE | 7→8
         Route22 OFFICER·8badges.
 JOHTO:  0→1 Azalea GUIDE | Azalea gym ROCKET (beat Slowpoke Well, local) | 1→2 Ilex
         CUT tree | Goldenrod florist → SQUIRTBOTTLE (local); 2→3 Route36 SUDOWOODO
         (Squirtbottle) | 3→4 SURF to Cianwood | 4→5 Olivine GUIDE | 5→6 Mahogany
         GUIDE | Mahogany gym ROCKET (beat Rocket HQ, local); 6→7 Blackthorn GUIDE |
         7→8 Route26 OFFICER·8badges.
 HOENN:  0→1 BRINEY won't sail | 1→2 Mauville GUIDE | 2→3 Lavaridge GUIDE | 3→4
         Petalburg/Norman GUIDE | 4→5 SURF + Route120 KECLEON (Devon Scope, local) |
         5→6 SURF to Mossdeep + gym GUIDE | 6→7 DIVE to Sootopolis | 7→8 Ever Grande
         OFFICER·8badges + beat ARCHIE at the Seafloor Cavern (local).

 REMEMBER: every "→ next zone" arrow above also requires the SAME gym cleared in the
 other two regions. Portal from any gym town to its two same-tier siblings to do it.

 NO SOFT-LOCKS:
   • Every key item (POKe FLUTE, SQUIRTBOTTLE, DEVON SCOPE) is obtainable on the near
     side of the obstacle it opens; no obstacle can wall you in — only open area.
   • The shared tier can only ever RISE, and a region can lead it by at most one gym,
     so an unlocked area never re-locks.
   • From any gym town you can always reach the other two regions' current gym towns
     (via the portal triangles) — so you can always advance the interleave.
   Verified by tests/blockers_test.mjs, tests/crossregion_test.mjs, and the 72
   cross-region reachability checks in tests/quest_reach.mjs.
