# Poketactica — build plan

The Disgaea × Pokémon isometric tactics game (owner named it 2026-11-20; folder `poketactica/`). Research and the owner's decisions:
`Plans/DISGAEA_TACTICS_RESEARCH.md` §6. House rules every stage respects: `Plans/NEW_GAME_RULES.md`.

## The design in one page (owner, 2026-11-20)
- **An isometric grid with height**, in MicaSurvivor 3D's look (3D terrain, 2D PMD sprites, the Emerald move animations).
- **Disgaea turns**: your phase — move each unit (instant, undoable until it acts), queue its attack; **Execute**
  resolves the queue **in the order you queued it**, with the combo bonus; then the enemy phase.
- **Units are FIGURINES**: Pokémon only, **6 on the map**, deployed from a base tile. A figurine is an item: species,
  level **1–999** (no reincarnation), **1 ability** (one of its species' real abilities, rolled when it is found), **4
  equipped moves** and its **move pool** (every move it ever learned; the 4 are swapped from the pool **between battles**
  only). **No EVs, IVs or natures.**
- **Pokémon rules underneath**: the real damage formula, type chart, STAB, stat stages, statuses, weather, abilities,
  held items. Disgaea's additions on top: height, facing, combos, team attacks, lift & throw (by some Pokémon), Geo panels
  and symbols, throw-to-capture.
- **Speed** (owner, 2026-11-20) does two jobs, since phases don't use it for turn order: **Move range** (tiles per
  turn, from Speed tiers) and **evasion** (Disgaea's HIT/SPD: a hit's chance is the move's accuracy scaled by the
  attacker's Speed against the defender's, capped both ways). Not chosen: counters, hit-and-run, priority reactions,
  speed-ordered enemy phase.
- **PP and SP, both limits** (owner): every move keeps its own **PP**, and every use also costs **SP** from the unit's
  shared SP pool (stronger moves cost more). A move needs both left. Not chosen: weight, EV-yield aptitudes, bonds.
- **Getting figurines**: in the overworld (hidden items and item balls, a chance on catching, shops and prize corners,
  trainer and gym rewards) and in battle (throw a weakened enemy onto your base tile).
- **v1**: a hand-built story campaign + an Item-World-style generated dungeon. Single player; PvP later.

## Ground rules for the build
- **One rules source.** The Pokémon maths lives in one pure module shared with the overworld's `battle.js` (parity
  tests), not a third copy (`pvpbattle.js` already drifted).
- **Seeded and deterministic from the first line.** `engine(seed, actions)` replays exactly: resume, replays, spectating
  and bets are recipes (as Pair of Pears does).
- **Engine first, pure, node-tested**; the page only draws a `view()`.
- Every stage is one or more PRs from `origin/main`, gated, merged when the owner says. The game joins the Games switcher
  only at the end (stage 8), when `game_rules_test` passes for it; until then it is reachable by URL for playtesting.

## Stage 1 — Shared Pokémon rules core
- New `overworld/rules/` (pure ES modules, no DOM, no `Math.random`): type chart + `effectiveness`, `calcStat` / stage
  multipliers, `statusFx` / immunities, the damage pieces of `useMove` (base formula, crit, STAB, type, the `envMult`
  ability / item / weather / terrain multipliers, random roll), accuracy, priority. Every function takes an `rng`.
- `battle.js` calls the shared functions for the parts that are identical; **parity tests** run the old and new paths over
  thousands of seeded cases (species × moves × abilities × items) and must match exactly.
- Tests: `rules_core_test.mjs` (node), `rules_parity_test.mjs`.
- *Risk*: `battle.js` is the overworld's live battle. Mitigation: extract only pure arithmetic; no behaviour change; the
  parity tests plus the existing `battle_*` suites gate it.

## Stage 2 — Figurines and move shapes (data)
- **Figurine model** (`poketactica/js/figurine.js`): `{ id, species, shiny, level, exp, ability, moves[4], pool[], heldItem? }`.
  Stats: Pokémon's formula with IV = EV = 0 and no nature — `HP = ⌊2B·L/100⌋ + L + 10`, others `⌊2B·L/100⌋ + 5`
  (level 999, base 100 ≈ 2,000 — readable, no bloat). Exp curve to 999 (a new curve; the overworld's is level³ to 100).
- **Learnset stretch**: a move learned at Pokémon level `l` is learned at figurine level `⌈l × 9.99⌉` (so level-100 moves
  arrive at 999); evolution levels stretch the same way. Learned moves go into the pool; the player picks the 4.
- **Move shapes** (`poketactica/js/data/shapes.json`, generated by `tools/poketactica/gen_shapes.mjs` then hand-tuned):
  `move → { range, shape: single|line|cone|cross|radius|self|ally|tiles|map, size, friendly, contact }`, seeded from the
  category and `SPREAD_MOVES` / `ALL_ADJACENT` / contact / pulse / beam sets (research §4.3). A test checks all 882 moves
  have a shape.
- **Move / Jump / evasion per species**: Move from a Speed tier (e.g. 3–6); evasion from Speed (vs the attacker's,
  capped); Jump from type (Flying / Levitate ignore height, Ground / Rock / Fighting climb high; weight is not a stat);
  **who can lift and throw**: a list (Strength / Fighting / big Psychic users), lift range 1, throw range by Attack tier.
- **SP**: a pool per figurine (grows with level), refilled at the start of each battle; each move's SP cost from its
  power / effect tier, alongside its PP.

## Stage 3 — The tactics engine (node, no page)
- `poketactica/js/engine/`: map (height grid, tiles, base tile, Geo colours), units, **phases + queue + Execute**, movement
  (breadth-first over the height grid with Move / Jump; undo until acted), attacks through the shapes and the shared rules
  core, **height ±10% per level, facing (side ×1.25 / back ×1.67 accuracy)**, **combo +10% per earlier hit in the Execute
  (capped ×2)**, **team attacks** (adjacent allies join a normal attack; % shown), **lift & throw**, statuses / weather /
  terrains as Geo colours / hazards as tile traps, KO and retreat to base, **throw-to-capture** (odds from the real catch
  rate and HP, shown before the throw), exp and level-ups into the move pool, win / lose / objectives.
- **Geo panels and symbols** with chains (repaint spiral, damage, symbol chain, Bonus Gauge 0–9).
- **AI**: Disgaea's patterns (groups passive until you enter their reach, bosses hold, aggressors go for nearest / KO)
  with the overworld's move scoring over (tile, move, target).
- `view()` / `label()` / `snapshot()`; engine fuzz test (random legal turns never break invariants); replay test
  (`seed + actions` → identical end state).

## Stage 4 — The page: 3D board and Balatro HUD
- `poketactica/index.html` on MicaSurvivor 3D's renderer: `render.js` terrain / water / sprites / shadows, `fx.js` move
  animations, `props.js` for Geo symbols. Shared bits pulled out of `micasurvivor/m3d/main.js` (frame loop, sheet
  loading, damage numbers, hit-stop) into a small shared module both use.
- **New**: an orthographic camera that rotates in 90° steps (Q / E) with zoom; tile highlights (move range, attack area,
  throw arc, Geo colours); HP bars over units; Attack / Hurt / Faint sheets played (on disk, unused today); turn queue
  strip; damage / capture-odds previews.
- **Balatro HUD** (DOM over the canvas, `runui.js` pattern): unit panel (stats, ability, 4 moves), action menu (Move,
  Attack, Lift / Throw, Wait, Execute, End phase), team / move-pool screen between battles, Bonus Gauge.
- **Controls** (`startInput`): arrows / d-pad move the tile cursor, Z = A select / confirm, X = B cancel / undo move,
  C = X unit info, V = Y Execute, Q / E rotate, Enter menu; touch taps tiles and buttons; portrait and landscape phones.
- Tests: page boots, a scripted battle played keyboard-only, pad-only and touch-only, no page errors.

## Stage 5 — Figurines in the overworld and the inventory
- A figurine collection kept on the account (server row like Pocket's collection), so trade and bets can hold them.
- Overworld sources, each with its own odds table and tests:
  - hidden items / item balls (a figurine placed in the map item tables),
  - **catch drop** (`ow_figurine.js`, the `ow_pocketcard.js` pattern: a chance on catching, that species' figurine),
  - shops and prize corners (priced in stars / coins),
  - trainer and gym leader rewards.
- Universal Inventory tab + **trade kind** (per-figurine, like a Pokémon) + **star value** in `site/values.js` (by level /
  species rarity) so they can be traded and bet.
- **A figurine unlocks its species in MicaSurvivor** (owner, 2026-11-20): a new `figurine` source in MicaSurvivor's
  one unlock list (`micasurvivor/js/systems/unlocks.js`, beside gacha / starters / overworld / expedition). Owning a
  figurine adds that species (its regional forme where it has one; never the family) to every MicaSurvivor mode. Like every
  other source it only adds: trading the figurine away later keeps the unlock. Read from the account's figurine
  collection on the server (a guest: this device's), at the same sync that reads the overworld's catches. Tested like
  `micasurvivor_unlocks_test.mjs`.

## Stage 6 — Campaign v1
- A short story campaign of hand-built maps (target ~10): teaching maps (move, attack, Execute, combos, throw, capture),
  Geo puzzles, height puzzles, a boss per chapter. Rewards pay **stars** and figurines.
- Map format: a JSON height grid + tiles + Geo + enemy figurine lists, validated by a test (reachable, base tile clear).

## Stage 7 — The generated dungeon (Item-World style)
- Endless generated floors from a seed (`gen.js`-style generator for tactics maps), harder per floor; boss floors every
  10 with exits; random Geo layouts; the main source of exp and figurines after the campaign. Floor rewards in stars.

## Stage 8 — Watch, replay, bets, and joining the site
- Live spectating (`publish-poketactica` / `poketactica-state`, the Micatro channel), replays as `seed + actions` recipes
  (`?replay=`) with clip links, spectator bets (`MARK.poketactica`, `prog` = turn, `res` = win / lose).
- Register everywhere `game_rules_test` asks: switcher, `switcher_games_lib` PAGES, `game_rules.mjs` row (no waivers),
  LANES, `SOLO_LIVE`, livewatch, watch hub, replaylib, home tile, sitemap.
- Full gate (`[full gate]`), then the owner merges.

## Later (not v1)
PvP and co-op on a map; Evility-style extra passive slots; Dark-Assembly-style unlocks; more campaign chapters.

## Questions still open
- Exp curve shape to 999 and how fast figurines level (to tune in stage 3 with a balance sim).
- Whether held items carry over from the overworld's item list (106 held effects exist).
