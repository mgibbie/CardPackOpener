========================================================================
FOREST POOL DESIGN NOTES  (Green / G advanced-land pool)
========================================================================
Purpose: a working reference for redesigning the 70-card Forest pool so it
reads as a cohesive GREEN toolbox — consistent with MTG's green color pie
AND buildable with mechanics that already exist in this game's engine.

What the Forest pool IS: 70 uncollectible, mono-green, no-rarity cards. A
Forest-based deck "Discovers" from this set (see landSet:'Forest'), so the
pool should feel like green's answer to every situation: ramp, fat beasts,
counters, fights, trample finishers, adaptation. Every card is colors:['G'],
collectible:false, no rarity, landSet:'Forest'. Keep those invariants — the
pool tests (pool_forest_test / abzan_pool_test family) enforce them.

Everything below is grounded in what's actually implemented: keyword tags
live in battlecards/keywords.js; effect "type" strings are registered
handlers in battlecards/engine/effects/*.js. If it's named here, it works.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GREEN'S COLOR-PIE LANE  (what green is allowed to do well)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
GREEN DOES:
  - Big, efficient creatures (best stats-per-mana of any color)
  - Trample / combat dominance
  - Ramp (mana acceleration, extra mana crystals, lands-matter)
  - +1/+1 counters and growth
  - Fight (creature-vs-creature removal) — green does NOT get clean burn
  - Adapt / evolve (roll upgrades onto a creature)
  - Beasts, Elves, Wurms, and other natural tribes; creatures-matter payoffs
  - Reach-style big blockers (Taunt) and haste on beasts (Rush)
  - Naturalize — destroy artifacts / enchantments (and now locations)
  - Nature's bounty: gain Life, Regenerate, heal, Food (Cook)
  - Protection for its own things: Hexproof / Ward
  - Card advantage tied to CREATURES ("draw for each creature", draw a Beast)

GREEN DOES NOT (push these elsewhere; flag if seen in the pool):
  - Direct damage to face / burn ............. Red
  - Unconditional single-target "Destroy target creature" ... Black
    (green removal must be conditional: Fight, destroy the biggest,
     destroy an attacker/flyer, or trade in combat)
  - Card draw for its own sake, tutoring any card ... Blue/Black
  - Mill / self-mill as a plan ............... Blue/Black
  - Counterspells, bounce, Freeze ............ Blue
  - Discard ................................... Black/Red
  - Divine Shield, Lifesteal, mass buffs of tokens ... White (Alliance = G+W)
  - Stealth, Windfury, evasion-as-a-theme .... Blue/Rogue/Red
  - Spell Damage / "spells matter" ........... Blue/Red

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. KEYWORDS  (tag string in keywords.js) — sorted by fit
------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORE GREEN — use freely, these define the pool:
  trample ....... excess lethal carries to the hero. Green's signature.
  adapt ......... "Battlecry: Adapt" (roll a d10 x3, pick one upgrade).
                  Effect shape: {type:'adapt', target:'self'} (or
                  'friendly-creature' / 'friendly-creatures', or times:N).
  landfall ...... lands-matter payoff (pair with ramp effects below).
  deathtouch .... green/black "big-game hunter"; great on small bodies.
  poisonous ..... damaged creature dies at end of turn; green/black.
  venomous ...... one-shot deathtouch variant; use sparingly.
  taunt ......... green's reach/vigilance analog — fat wall blockers.
  rush .......... haste on beasts (R/G). Fine on aggressive green.
  ward .......... "Ward N" — opponent pays extra to target it. Green/blue
                  protection; good on your ramp dorks and finishers.
  proliferate ... adds to existing counters (G+U). Pairs with +1/+1 kit.
  regenerate .... nature's resilience (via 'regenerate' effect/keyword).
  frenzy ........ one-time trigger the first time it survives damage —
                  green "bloodrush"/enrage flavor (grow when hurt).
  overkill ...... bonus when it overkills on your turn — trample synergy.
  emerge ........ big-creature discount from hand; green fatties.
  colossal ...... enters huge with Appendage tokens (buff-colossal /
                  grant-colossal-parts exist). Reserve for top-end.

SITUATIONAL — on-pie in small doses, don't overuse:
  first_strike (shows as "Swift") ... ok on fighters; more W/R, keep rare.
  cleave / piercing / slashing ...... trample-adjacent combat; a few only.
  scry .............................. mild card selection; "Ranger" flavor.
  inspire ........................... after Hero Power; already used in pool.
  medic ............................. heal beside it end of turn (nature).
  coven ............................. 3 different-Attack creatures; go-tall.
  chromatic ......................... multicolor payoff; niche in a mono pool.
  cook .............................. Food -> heal (G+W nourishment).

OFF-PIE FOR GREEN — avoid in new designs; reconsider where present:
  static ....... paralyze-on-survive (currently on 1 pool beast). Blue/red
                 control feel; prefer Frenzy or Trample instead.
  elusive ...... tag doubles as "Hexproof". FINE when the intent is
                 Hexproof (protect your own). NOT fine as evasion/unblockable
                 (that's blue). Current pool has "Elusive & Trample" beasts —
                 reflavor those to Ward/Trample or plain Trample.
  divine_shield, lifesteal, freezer, stealth, windfury, spell-damage,
  firebreathing, sanguine, secret ... all off-pie; move to their colors.
  (Reito Sentinel's "target player mills 3" is owner-requested but mill is
   off-pie blue/black — treat as a one-off, don't multiply it.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EFFECTS  (registered handler 'type' strings) grouped by green theme
------------------------------------------------------------------------
RAMP / LANDS-MATTER (very green — the pool is currently light on this):
  gain-empty-mana-crystal ..... "Battlecry: Gain an empty Mana Crystal."
  gain-mana ................... temporary mana this turn (Landfall payoff).
  gain-max-mana / set-own-max-mana ... permanent ramp (top-end only).
  temp-mana / temp-crystal-next-turn ... burst ramp.
  (Landfall card already does {type:'gain-mana'} on a land drop — lean in.)

+1/+1 COUNTERS & GROWTH:
  add-counters ................ put +1/+1 counter(s) on a creature.
  grow ....................... permanent self-buff over time.
  proliferate ................ multiply existing counters.
  buff / buff-self / buff-target / buff-target-and-adjacent
  adjacent-buff / bolster (buff weakest) / buff-all-tribe
  buff-friendly-tribe / buff-hand-tribe (Beast-lord "anthem" effects)
  buff-random-friendly / buff-self-per-other-friendly (go-wide payoff)

FIGHT & GREEN-LEGAL REMOVAL (never clean "destroy target creature"):
  fight ...................... your creature fights theirs (core green).
  fight-random-beast ......... beast-flavored fight.
  destroy-strongest / destroy-weaker-minion ... "hunt the biggest".
  destroy-permanent .......... Naturalize; use target
                               'artifact-or-enchantment' or
                               'artifact-location-or-enchantment'
                               (Reclamation Sage uses the latter).
  destroy-art-ench ........... random artifact/enchantment removal.
  damage-target-by-attack .... "deal damage equal to a creature's Attack"
                               (green's fight-as-a-spell).

BEASTS / CREATURES-MATTER (green tribal glue):
  summon / summon-with-stats / fill-board-token ... make Beast/Insect tokens.
  summon-from-deck-tribe / recruit ... pull creatures from your deck.
  summon-died-tribe / resurrect-tribe ... regrow fallen beasts.
  discover (tribe:'Beast') ... green "tutor a creature" (already used).
  draw-beast-gain-stats / draw-minion-tribe-then ... beast card advantage.
  hatch-egg / summon-hatch-egg ... token/beast generation flavor.

CARD ADVANTAGE (green-legal: tie it to creatures/lands, not raw):
  draw with a per-creature count ("Draw a card for each creature you
    control" already exists as a sorcery) — this is the green way.
  return-from-graveyard / gy-return ... regrow a creature (green recursion).
  draw-beast-gain-stats ...... conditional, on-pie.
  (Avoid flat "draw 3" as the pool's identity — it's fine as one blue-splash
   outlier but shouldn't define green.)

LIFE / NATURE'S BOUNTY:
  heal / heal-full / heal-adjacent-full / heal-hero-full
  gain life via {type:'heal', target:'self'/'hero'} (Wurm lifegain)
  regenerate ................. resilience
  cook ...................... Food token -> later heal (G+W)
  medic (keyword) ........... passive end-of-turn heal of neighbors

BIG FINISHERS / TOP-END (green closes games with size, not burn):
  Cost-reducers that scale with your board: the pool's 12/12 "Costs X less,
    X = total Attack you control, Trample" is the model — replicate this
    pattern (buff-self-per-*, discount-by-board) for other fatties.
  colossal / emerge fatties, trample + overkill, spend-mana-double-stats.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TRIBES  (keep green-flavored; retag the off-color ones)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON-FLAVOR (keep / expand): Beast, Elf (Shaman/Scout/Knight/Warrior/
  Ranger/Druid), Wurm, Insect, Wolf, Bear, Boar, Ape, Spider, Hydra,
  Dinosaur, Treefolk, Dryad, Fungus (Saproling tokens), Centaur, Elemental,
  Snake/Naga (nature-ish), Spirit (nature spirits ok).
OFF-FLAVOR IN A GREEN POOL (reconsider): Mech / Mech Beast / Mech Construct
  (Mechs are artifact/colorless — either reflavor to Beast/Construct-of-
  living-wood or accept as a deliberate "wildlife-machine" oddball; don't
  add more). "Archer" subtypes are fine as flavor.
NOTE: several current cards are just "Beast" — that's fine, but vary the
  creature types to make the pool feel like an ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ARCHETYPES the pool should support (pick lanes per card)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A) RAMP -> FATTIES: mana dorks / Landfall ramp that pay off into
     Trample/Colossal/Emerge finishers. (Pool is short on the ramp half.)
  B) +1/+1 COUNTERS: add-counters + proliferate + grow + Adapt, with an
     anthem or two (buff-friendly-tribe). "Go tall."
  C) BEASTS TRIBAL: Beast-lords (buff-hand-tribe / buff-friendly-tribe),
     Discover a Beast, summon/recruit beasts, draw-beast-gain-stats.
  D) STOMP / COMBAT: Trample + Rush + Frenzy + Overkill + Fight removal.
     Green's "I have the bigger creatures, now what?" answer.
  E) GO-WIDE (light G+W splash): token makers + adjacent/mass buffs +
     Alliance-style payoffs. Keep small so it stays green, not white.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. POWER LEVEL & STRUCTURAL NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - These are pool cards a deck DISCOVERS; keep rates fair (roughly on-curve
    vanilla + one keyword, or a modest body + one effect). The 12/12 cost-
    scaler and 7/7 lifegain wurm are the intended ceiling.
  - Preserve invariants on every card: type creature/spell/etc., colors:['G'],
    collectible:false, NO rarity field, landSet:'Forest', set:'wubrg'.
    Adding/removing cards changes the count (pool_forest_test expects 70) and
    can only be colored/uncollectible members — don't introduce a collectible
    or a second color.
  - Editing existing pool cards is SAFE for seeded tests (they're
    uncollectible, so they're not in the collectible default-deck RNG). Only
    ADD/REMOVE with care.
  - Keyword text convention: multiple plain keywords join with " & " and end
    with "." (e.g. "Trample & Poisonous."); a triggered ability goes on its
    own line (e.g. "Trample.\nBattlecry: Adapt."). Keyword names in
    description must match keywords.js display words (e.g. "Swift" for
    first_strike, "Hexproof"/"Elusive" for elusive).
  - Verify by FIRING, not by inspecting JSON: instantiate the card, play it
    or kill it, and assert the mechanic resolved (see the owner_todo_cardsN
    regression tests for the pattern). Keyword arrays ARE what combat reads.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CURRENT POOL — QUICK OBSERVATIONS (redesign opportunities)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - HEAVILY vanilla: ~20+ cards are plain "Trample." beasts of assorted
    stats. That's a lot of dead texture. Keep a handful as honest green
    beaters; convert the rest into the archetypes above (ramp, counters,
    beast synergy, fight).
  - Under-served green identity: RAMP (only ~2 mana-crystal cards) and
    +1/+1 COUNTERS (only the artifact "{T}: put a +1/+1 counter"). These are
    green's backbone — add more.
  - Off-pie to revisit: "Rush & Static" beast (static), the "Elusive &
    Trample" beasts (evasion), any Mech tribes, and any flat unconditional
    "Destroy target creature" (make it a Fight or a "destroy the biggest").
  - Good models already present to imitate: "Costs X less = total Attack,
    Trample" finisher; "Deathtouch & Rush" hunter; "Battlecry: Adapt";
    "Deathrattle: Discover a Beast"; the +1/+1-counter artifact; Landfall
    "gain 1 mana"; "fight" sorcery; Naturalize variants.
