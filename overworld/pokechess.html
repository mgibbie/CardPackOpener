⚙️ Games
Click a piece, then click a highlighted square. Each piece type is one of your Pokémon; capturing an enemy piece triggers a battle — win and you take the square, lose and your attacker is defeated.
Keys: arrows / WASD move · Z (or Enter) pick · X deselect · C replays · V twice resign · Backspace leave · hold Esc or ` for the Games switcher. A controller works too (Select leaves, Start + Select switches games).
