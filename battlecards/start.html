Welcome to Battlecards

A deep collectible card game — thousands of cards, thirteen classes, and a full deck builder. Play quick matches against the AI, dive into run modes, or challenge your friends.

⚔️

How a turn works

Each turn you gain a Mana crystal, play creatures and spells, and attack. Reduce your opponent from 40 life to 0. Lands, keywords, and class powers add the depth.

Read the full rules →

🏞️

Lands & color identity

Click an empty land slot to develop a basic land for 3 mana — each taps for a colored boost. The colors your lands make are your color identity, and they unlock advanced lands with powerful themed card pools. Wastes tap for damage instead of a color.

🖐️

Your hand

You can hold up to 15 cards — but at the end of your turn you must discard down to the limit, so don't hoard forever. Killing things, drawing big, and Discovering all feed the hand; spend it!
