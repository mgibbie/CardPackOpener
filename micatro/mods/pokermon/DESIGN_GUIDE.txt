POKERMON JOKER DESIGN GUIDE
A study of the existing Pokermon jokers, for designing the 445 Pokemon that don't have one yet
==================================================================================================
Prepared 2026-10-27.

Sources (read only; nothing in the repo was changed):
  - micatro/mods/pokermon        Pokermon 3.9.1 port (pin 3.9.1+r18): jokers/*.js, data.js + the
                                 data391.js overlay, systems/*.js, PORTING.md
  - micatro/mods/nacho           Nacho's Pokermon Dip 1.7.9-5
  - micatro/mods/pokermonplus    Sonfive's Pokermon+ 1.2.0
  - upstream Pokermon 3.9.1 Lua  (localization/en-us.lua, pokermon.lua, pokesprites.lua)
  - designwiki/data/pokermon_jokers.json (PR #825): the 445 missing species, with type, stage,
    rarity/cost guesses and evolution lines

How the numbers were made:
  The three mods were loaded in node through the same index.js files the game uses. Every
  registered joker center was dumped (ptype, stage, rarity, cost, config), and its text was
  resolved through its own loc_vars, so the numbers quoted are the numbers the card shows.
  "Feature" counts come from scanning the resolved text plus the config field names. "Hook"
  counts come from scanning calculate() and the local helpers it calls, so they are approximate.
  A shared helper can mention a context the joker never really uses, and end_of_round is
  inflated by the evolution checks.
  Legacy (retired) add-on jokers are excluded. "Non-Mega" means the 610 Pokemon jokers, forms
  included (Rotom forms, Unown, Tauros (Herd), Dreepy Dart and so on).

Conventions used in this file:
  Stage 1 / Stage 2 = Pokermon's "One" / "Two".
  Rarity: C = Common, U = Uncommon, R = Rare, L = Legendary, S = Safari, M = Mega.
  Numbers written a-b~m mean: min a, max b, median m.

1. OVERVIEW
==================================================================================================

1.1 What a Pokermon joker is made of
------------------------------------
Every Pokemon joker is a center with these parts. Field names are the ones used in data.js and
in defineJokers.

  ptype       One of 12 types: Grass, Fire, Water, Lightning, Psychic, Fighting, Colorless,
              Dark, Metal, Fairy, Dragon, Earth. The internal name is "Dark"; its Energy card is
              called "Darkness Energy". Two oddities: Missingno is "Bird", and Nacho's Terapagos
              is "Stellar".
              The type drives Energy matching, type synergies ("each Dark Joker..."), Tera
              stickers, the card frame and the attribute <type>_type.
  stage       Baby / Basic / One / Two / Legendary / Mega. "Other" is used for non-Pokemon
              jokers (Pokedex, Everstone, Rival...), and Shedinja is "???".
  rarity      1-4 (Common to Legendary), 'poke_safari', or 'poke_mega'.
  cost        The shop price. The sell value is half of it, as in vanilla.
  config      extra = { the numbers }, plus evo_rqmt for scaling evolutions. Field names matter,
              because Energy only scales certain names (see 2.4).
  ability     calculate(self, card, context). Most jokers have one effect, some have two
              boxes, each fired on one of the engine's contexts.
  evolution   One of:
                level_evo     rounds countdown
                scaling_evo   reach evo_rqmt
                item_evo      item_req
                type_evo      a Tera sticker
                deck_*_evo    a deck composition
                a custom condition
  scaling     A growing value (mult with mult_mod, chips with chip_mod, Xmult with Xmult_mod).
              It carries over when the joker evolves.
  energy      Using a matching Energy card permanently raises the numeric fields by a share of
              their base values (up to 3 times per joker by default).
  text        A loc entry j_poke_<name>, with the evolution footer in small grey text, for
              example "(Evolves after 4 rounds)".
  optional    attributes (3.9.1 Steamodded attributes), megas, starter, volatile,
              enhancement_gate (pool gate), a held item created in add_to_deck,
              poke_custom_values_to_keep.

Worked example: Charmander (Fire, Basic, Uncommon, $5)
  config    { evo_rqmt: 16, extra: { d_size: 1, mult: 0, mult_mod: 1 } }
  text      "+1 discard / Gains +1 Mult if hand is played when 0 discards remaining /
            (Evolves at +0 / +16 Mult)"
  hooks     before (gain), joker_main (score), add_to_deck (discards)
  evolution scaling_evo -> Charmeleon (Safari $8, +2 a step, evolves at 36), then Charizard
            (Safari $10: +36 Mult, and X1.5 when 0 discards remain)
  energy    mult (+40% of base) and mult_mod (+20%) grow; d_size does not.

  - Legendaries and mythicals: most of Gens 4-9 (the Regis, the Lati twins and the weather trio are

    yet.
  - Paradox Pokemon: all 20 are missing. Each is a Rare single-stage Basic in the wiki's guess.

2. SYSTEMS PRIMER
==================================================================================================

2.1 Types and the ptype mapping
-------------------------------
The 12 Pokermon types are POKETYPES in systems/types.js.

OWNER RULE (2026-10-28): the type shown on the CARD is always right. Upstream 3.9.1 draws a finished
card for every species the wiki lists, with the type icon in its bottom-left corner (and the frame
colour). A new joker takes that type, even where it differs from the mapping below: Latias and
Latios are Psychic, Venipede Dark, Sandile Dark, Kyurem Water, Stunfisk Lightning, Keldeo
Fighting, Urshifu Single Strike Dark and Rapid Strike Water. The wiki's card shows it ("Pokermon
type ... as its card shows"; pokermon_jokers.json type_source 'art'), read from the icon by
tools/micatro/pokermon_art_type.mjs (template matching trained on the 552 upstream jokers, 100%
leave-one-out). 66 of the 402 wiki cards differ from the mapping.

The mapping is only the FALLBACK, for a species with no upstream card (type_source 'mapping'). It
maps the real types like this:
  Normal  -> Colorless       Bug      -> Grass       Electric -> Lightning
  Ice     -> Water           Poison   -> Dark        Ghost    -> Psychic
  Ground  -> Earth           Rock     -> Earth       Steel    -> Metal
  Fire, Water, Grass, Fighting, Psychic, Dragon, Dark and Fairy map to themselves.
  A species whose first type is Flying takes its second type. Pure Flying becomes Colorless.

Existing lines sometimes change type when the species gains one:
  Seedot (Grass)     -> Nuzleaf (Dark)
  Pineco (Grass)     -> Forretress (Metal)
  Pupitar (Earth)    -> Tyranitar (Dark)
  Seadra (Water)     -> Kingdra (Dragon)
  Onix (Earth)       -> Steelix (Metal)
  Grubbin (Grass)    -> Charjabug (Lightning)
  Bunnelby (Colorless) -> Diggersby (Earth)
Use this when the new type opens a better identity. Some Megas change type too: Mega Charizard
X and Mega Ampharos become Dragon, Mega Lopunny and Mega Mewtwo X become Fighting.

Type synergy is read with find_pokemon_type(type) or is_type(card, type). Tera stickers
(<type>_sticker) override a joker's type and can sit on vanilla jokers.

House pattern: a WEATHER / FORM change (the owner's Castform, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js):
  - The joker reads the weather once a round, on the round's FIRST deal: Steamodded's hand_drawn
    context while G.STATE is DRAW_TO_HAND with nothing played or discarded yet (and once per
    G.GAME.round). Not first_hand_drawn: in Micatro that context runs before the deal's draw
    events land, so the hand is still empty there. Not Blueprint.
  - The form (ability.extra.form) holds for the whole round and picks the effect; later draws
    (after a play or a discard) never re-read it. After the round it is Normal again (queued in the
    end_of_round pass, so the other jokers' end-of-round effects still see the form): in the shop
    it is its own type.
  - Its TYPE follows the form by changing its own ability.extra.ptype, which is_type,
    find_pokemon_type, get_type and energy_matches all read. No Tera sticker, so a player's Tera
    still overrides it as on any joker. card.set_debuff() is re-run and Kecleons are re-synced (they
    copy the joker to their right) whenever it changes, as Kecleon's own set_type does.
  - Energy: one field per form (mult1 / chips1 / Xmult_multi / mult2), so every Energy raises
    every form at once. Its current type's Energy matches while it is in that form; Colorless
    Energy always matches (full strength only while it is Colorless, half otherwise: energize's
    usual rule for a non-Colorless ptype).
  - Monotype exemption: a center with poke_mono_exempt: true is left out of every Monotype check
    (systems/runbadges.js mono_state): it never breaks the run, never locks the type, never
    triggers the shop warning. Use it only where the type is outside the player's control (the
    weather) or changes on its own every round (the owner's Burmy, whose cloak follows the deck: the
    owner's ruling); the Nuzlocke reads the joker as usual. Say it on the card ("its type never breaks
    Monotype"). An evolution that LOCKS the changing type (Wormadam) is not exempt: it is checked like
    any joker (its gained type, the Basic's, or its current one must match).
  - Art: one cell per form when upstream has one (Castform's Sunny / Rainy / Snowy sit on the 3.9.1
    Gen 3 sheet, unlisted in pokespriteload.lua), drawn by ui.js ownFace from the view's form.
  - Darmanitan's Zen Mode is the other shape: a temporary Tera sticker, restored at end of round.

House pattern: a TWO-TYPE TEAM count (the owner's Barboach line, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js muddy_count):
  - "for each Water or Earth Joker": a joker counts when is_type(j, 'Water') || is_type(j, 'Earth'), so its ptype, a Tera
    sticker (which REPLACES the type: apply_type_sticker rewrites extra.ptype too), Castform's current form and an add-on's
    IS_TYPE_RULES (Pokermon+'s Grafaiai) all count with no special case. A joker that is both counts ONCE (one test per joker,
    never find_pokemon_type('Water').length + find_pokemon_type('Earth').length).
  - "itself included": count the others, then +1, so the shop / collection card shows the number it gives once bought.
  - Debuffed jokers don't count (!j.debuff). This DIFFERS from the older Water counters: Corsola, Pelipper and Politoed use
    find_pokemon_type, which counts a debuffed joker too. Say which one a new design uses.
  - A threshold on OTHER jokers ("at least 2 other Water or Earth Jokers") leaves the joker itself out, so one copy alone never
    turns it on.

House pattern: a PAIR with half-strength cross-coverage (the owner's Lunatone + Solrock,
jokers/pokejokers_designs.js; Plusle / Minun, pokejokers_11.js, are the older pair):
  - Each one alone covers HALF of a cycle: Solrock +100 Chips on day hands, Lunatone X2 on night
    hands. The cycle is the round's hand count: G.GAME.current_round.hands_played before the hand
    (0, 2, 4... = day: the 1st, 3rd and 5th hands; still the pre-hand count during scoring, run.js
    adds 1 after evaluate_play). It resets each round (new_round). Discards don't move it.
  - Holding BOTH (each checks find_card(partner), which skips debuffed cards: a debuffed partner
    breaks the pair; a Blueprint copy is not the partner) unlocks the other half at HALF strength:
    Solrock +50 Chips by night, Lunatone X1.5 by day, so every hand gets both. Each copy acts on
    its own (two Solrocks + a Lunatone give +200 / +100).
  - Energy: store only the full value (chips, Xmult) and DERIVE the half (chips / 2, and for an X
    1 + (X - 1) / 2), so one energy field raises both and the pair rule stays exact. Earth Energy:
    +130 / +65 Chips and X2.4 / X1.7 after one, +190 / +95 and X3.2 / X2.1 after three.
  - Price: Uncommon $6 for a single-stage Basic (the default is Rare $7), because each alone works
    half the time.
  - Tooltip: say which half the NEXT hand is ("Next hand: Day"; out of a round, i.e. in the shop, it is
    the next round's first hand: Day) and the pair status ("Cosmic Power: active / needs Lunatone").
House pattern: a ONE-TYPE TEAM count, the Colorless Bird flock (the owner's Starly line, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js flock_count):
  - The two-type count above with one type: is_type(j, 'Colorless') && !j.debuff, itself included (+1). A Tera sticker of another
    type takes a Colorless joker OUT (the sticker replaces the type); Castform counts only while Normal (in the shop, or on a tied
    forecast), never while Sunny / Rainy / Snowy. Miltank's older count (find_pokemon_type) still counts debuffed jokers.
  - The line: +2 / +4 Mult per Colorless Joker (Starly U $6, 4 rounds; Staravia S $8, 5 rounds), then the payoff moves from + to
    X (pattern 2.3 b): Staraptor S $10, X(1 + 0.5 per Colorless Joker). With 4 Colorless jokers that is +8 / +16 / X3.
  - Store the per-joker number in an energy field (mult +40%, Xmult_mod +20%: Gardevoir's per-count X field), so Energy raises
    every joker's share at once and is re-applied on evolution: 2 -> 2.8 / 3.6 / 4.4, 4 -> 5.6 / 7.2 / 8.8, X0.5 -> 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.8.
  - A DRAWBACK on a Stage 2 ("Recoil", Brave Bird's): each hand played loses $1, in a field Energy ignores (`recoil`). It goes
    into debt only as far as the run allows (dollars - 1 >= bankrupt_at: $0 normally, -$20 with Credit Card; Gholdengo's buffer
    check), so it never blocks a play and never strands the player below the shop's own limit. Not copied by Blueprint (it copies
    the X Mult only); a debuffed joker pays nothing. Say the limit on the card ("Never past your debt limit").
House pattern: a VOCABULARY of hand types (the owner's Chatot, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js vocabulary / new_word):
  - Count the DIFFERENT poker hands played this run from the run's own records: G.GAME.hands[h].played > 0 (run.js evaluate_play
    adds 1 before the jokers' `before` pass and never resets it in a run). So a joker bought late sees the whole run, secret hands
    (Five of a Kind, Flush House, Flush Five) and add-on hands count, and the hand being played already counts in joker_main.
    Chatot: +4 Mult per type (`mult`, Energy +40%: 5.6 / 7.2 / 8.8). A typical run plays 5-7 types, so +20 to +28 Mult at Rare $7.
  - A FIRST-TIME rider reads the same record: in `before`, `played === 1` is the hand's first play this run. Chatot creates that
    hand's Planet (P_CENTER_POOLS.Planet by config.hand_type) with consumable room (the Blue Seal's
    cards + consumeable_buffer < card_limit check), only while held: a type first played without it is past 1 and never grants.
    Keep a per-joker record of what it granted (`new_words`), so a retriggered `before` or a mod resetting a count can't grant
    twice. Not copied by Blueprint (it copies the Mult); a debuffed hand (The Psychic...) has no `before` pass, so no Planet.
  - Don't use played_this_round for a run count (it resets each round), and don't count from the purchase (a late buy is dead).
House pattern: a FIRST-HAND team X (the owner's Pachirisu, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js spark_x / pouch_money):
  - The round's first hand is current_round.hands_played === 0 while it scores (a discard before it doesn't use it up). Pachirisu:
    X2 Mult (`Xmult`) plus X0.25 (`Xmult_mod`) per OTHER Lightning Joker on that hand: X2 alone, X2.75 with three others. One
    hand a round, so the X runs higher than an always-on Basic X (Lightning Basic X 1.75-2.5). Energy +20% on both: X2.4 / 2.8 / 3.2
    and X0.3 / 0.35 / 0.4 per joker. The team count is Barboach / Starly's (is_type, not debuffed), but of the OTHERS for the X
    (it pays X2 on its own) and itself included for money.
  - Pair it with an end-of-round payout in a fixed field Energy ignores (`pouch`: $1 per Lightning Joker, itself included),
    through calc_dollar_bonus so it is a row of the cash-out list (Miltank's shape). Blueprint copies the X, never the payout
    (calc_dollar_bonus isn't copied, as Golden Joker); a debuffed joker pays nothing.
  - Tooltip: the current X and its state ("Active!" / "Used this round"; in the shop it is the next round's first hand) and the
    current payout. Juice the card on the first deal while it is on (Whiscash's Earthquake).

House pattern: a TRIO (the owner's Regirock / Regice / Registeel, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js regi_others):
  - Separate species (no family), each with its own rule, sharing one scaling rider: "1 + (OTHER Regis you have)". The count is of
    DISTINCT other species, undebuffed: a twin of the same Regi (Showman) is never an "other Regi", and two Regirocks count once
    for a Regice. All three held = 3 each; one alone = 1. Write the base in the tooltip's number, "+1 for each other Regi" below.
  - The rider is a DECK CONVERSION when a Blind is selected (Marble Joker's moment; Blueprint copies it): N random cards of the
    FULL deck (G.playing_cards, the run's seeded pseudorandom_element) become the joker's enhancement. Unenhanced cards first;
    once none is left, any card that isn't already that enhancement (so it overwrites, keeping seal and edition); never one that
    already is. Three converters on one deck end up trading cards with each other once the deck is fully enhanced: say so.
  - Rules that change a base enhancement belong on the CARD, through the engine's Card hooks, not in a joker context: Regirock's
    Stone "+100 instead of +50" is a chip_bonus hook (Card:get_chip_bonus), Registeel's held Steel "X2 instead of X1.5" a
    chip_h_x_mult hook (Card:get_chip_h_x_mult), so retriggers (Red Seal, Mime) repeat the new value exactly as they repeat the
    old one. Take the best copy's value (an "instead" rule never stacks with a twin); only while an undebuffed one is owned.
    The card's OWN tooltip must say the new number: answer the pure engine hook enh_loc_vars (describe.js enh_live_vars) with the
    same function the scoring hook uses (regi_enh_vars), so the Stone card reads +100 (+ its bank) and the Steel card X2, energized too.

House pattern: a WEATHER TRIO, clash and calm (the owner's Kyogre / Groudon / Rayquaza, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js primal_read):
  - Two rivals, each a full Legendary alone (Kyogre: +1 hand, scored Clubs retrigger; Groudon: +1 discard, scored Hearts / Diamonds
    X1.25), and each LOCKS Castform's weather (Rainy / Sunny) on its Forecast read.
  - CLASH (both held, undebuffed, no calmer): the round's weather is read ONCE, on the round's first deal (Castform's moment, 2.1),
    by whichever of the three reads first (a shared cache, G.GAME.poke_primal, so a Castform to their left sees the same weather).
    The side with more cards held wins the WHOLE round (Clubs vs Hearts + Diamonds, Castform's counting: Wild both, debuffed /
    Stone / Hazard neither). A tie goes to the one with more Energy, still tied to Kyogre (the listed first) — say the tie rule on
    the card. The loser does nothing that round, its +hand / +discard included: taken back at the read (never below 1 hand /
    0 discards), remembered on the joker so selling it that round doesn't take it twice; a loser gained mid-round gives nothing
    until the next round. The decision holds for the round whatever is bought or sold after it.
  - CALM (Rayquaza held, undebuffed): no clash, both work every round, and Castform reads its normal forecast (the weather is calmed
    rather than doubly locked). The calmer has its own scaling payoff (Dragon Ascent: X0.2 per hand containing a Straight or a
    Flush, once for a Straight Flush).
  - Energy: only the numbers that are numbers (Groudon's per-card X as Xmult_multi +5%; the calmer's gain as Xmult_mod +20%, its
    bank in a non-energy field). A rival with no number (Kyogre: +1 hand and a retrigger) is Energy-inert, marked
    poke_no_energy_field: true (the audit in pokermon_new_jokers_test needs one or the other).
  - Tooltip: the clash state ("Kyogre's round (4 Clubs, 1 red)", "calmed by Rayquaza", "no Groudon", "read on the next first deal").

House pattern: an EON DUO, a pair with full cross-growth (the owner's Latias / Latios, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js eon):
  - Each alone grows on HALF of a resource: Latias X0.15 per Tarot used, Latios X0.15 per Planet (context.using_consumeable,
    consumeable.ability.set). Holding BOTH (Soul Dew: find_card(partner), so a debuffed partner breaks it and a Blueprint is not
    the partner) every Tarot OR Planet grows both, each copy on its own. Unlike Lunatone / Solrock (half-strength cover) the duo
    unlocks the full gain: the partner is a second Legendary slot, priced like one.
  - Banked in eon_Xmult (no energy field), the gain Xmult_mod +20% per Energy (0.15 -> 0.18 / 0.21 / 0.24): Energy never raises
    what is banked. Blueprint copies the X, never the growth.

House pattern: a PER-ROUND FAVOURITE SUIT (the owner's Glameow line, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js fickle_pick):
  - Each joker keeps its OWN suit (two Glameows can like different suits), unlike the run-wide vanilla picks (Ancient Joker, The Idol,
    Castle: G.GAME.current_round.*_card). It is picked when the joker is first obtained (add_to_deck, if none is set) and again at
    each end of round (the round_end pass, not Blueprint), Ancient Joker's way: the run's seeded pseudorandom_element over the four
    suits MINUS the current favourite (so never the same twice running), seeded 'fickle' + ante.
  - Never pick on creation (set_ability): the shop and the collection create cards outside the run's recipe, so a draw there would
    move the seeded stream and break resume / replay. The unobtained card says "(Favourite: picked when obtained)".
  - Hold it on card.ability (fav_suit), not in ability.extra: Steamodded's set_ability keeps ability fields the new center doesn't set,
    so it rides through evolution with no keep-list, and the evolved form's add_to_deck sees it already set (no second draw).
  - Match with is_suit (Wild cards, Smeared Joker, debuffed cards never). Show it in the tooltip in the suit's colour ({V:1}).

House pattern: a JOKER SLOT AS A COST (the owner's Purugly, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js slot_cost_*):
  - -N Joker slots while held: G.jokers.config.card_limit in the center's add_to_deck / remove_from_deck (Banette's +1 the other way).
    Store N in a field Energy ignores (slot_cost). A Negative copy nets 0 (its own +1).
  - Keep the cost while DEBUFFED, as vanilla keeps a Negative's slot (queue_negative_removal): remove_from_deck(from_debuff) only
    queues the refund (ability.poke_slot_queued). Giving the slot back on a debuff would let lifting it put the jokers OVER the limit.
    A debuffed card that leaves never runs remove_from_deck again, so the card_removed hook (index.js) pays the queued refund.
  - An evolution INTO the cost must not overflow: evolve only with a free slot now (G.jokers.cards.length + joker_buffer < card_limit);
    otherwise wait at 0 rounds and evolve at the first end of round with room. Never push a joker out. Reserve the slot in
    G.GAME.joker_buffer between the decision and the queued swap (Riff-raff's way), so two ready jokers never both take the last
    slot. Say it on the card ("(Needs a free Joker slot to evolve)").
  - Pay it back with a strong per-card payoff: Purugly's X1.25 per scored favourite-suit card (Groudon's per-card X, but on one
    rotating suit and with a slot gone).

House pattern: a MATERIAL CLOAK, the deck picks the form (the owner's Burmy line, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js cloak_of_deck):
  - Castform's form pattern (above), read from the FULL DECK instead of the hand: at the end of each round (the main end_of_round
    pass, not Blueprint) the most common enhancement of G.playing_cards picks the cloak. Each card counts by its own enhancement
    (its center key; quantum enhancements don't), unenhanced being one kind and every other enhancement another, so "most common"
    means the single biggest kind (Stone 22 beats unenhanced 20 even with 10 Glass besides). Then the kind maps to a cloak: Stone
    Sandy (Earth), Steel Trash (Metal), unenhanced or anything else Plant (Grass).
  - A tie for the most between kinds that call for DIFFERENT cloaks keeps the current cloak (unenhanced tied with Glass is Plant
    either way, so it switches to Plant). An empty deck keeps it too.
  - The cloak is kept between rounds (unlike Castform's weather): it is the joker's state, ability.extra.form, which every
    evolution keeps (NAMES_TO_KEEP 'form' when the new center has one). Type and art follow it as Castform's: ex.ptype (a Tera
    sticker still owns the type: the cloak changes, the type stays the sticker's), one Gen 4 cell per cloak (systems/anim.js
    BURMY_SPRITES, ui.js ownFace).
  - Each cloak is a different PAYOFF of the same joker: Plant +5 Mult (`mult1`), Sandy +40 Chips (`chips1`), Trash $1 at end of round
    (calc_dollar_bonus, `trash_money`, fixed). The Energy fields of every cloak rise together; the $ never does. The cloak is
    re-read BEFORE the cash-out, so a deck that turned Steel this round already pays Trash's $1 this round.
  - The evolution LOCKS it: Wormadam keeps the cloak it wore on its last round (POST_EVOLVE re-applies the cloak's type after the kept
    values land) and never re-reads it; its three texts are per-cloak loc keys (loc_vars key, as Castform's forms).

House pattern: a GENDERED BRANCH (the owner's Burmy line; Burmy -> Wormadam Female / Mothim Male):
  - The gender is rolled ONCE, when the card is created (set_ability(initial): shop, pack, Pokeball, starting joker, create_card),
    with the run's seeded RNG (pseudorandom(pseudoseed('burmy_gender')) < 0.5), the shape of a Nature roll (2.5). It lives on
    ability.extra.gender, so it is saved with the card; a resume replays the recipe and rolls the same one. A copy (Invisible
    Joker, Ankh) keeps it (copy_card copies the ability).
  - The branch is a plain level_evo whose target is chosen by it (4 rounds: Female -> j_poke_wormadam, Male -> j_poke_mothim); no item,
    no counter. The tooltip names both and the card's own: "(Currently Plant Cloak, Male)".
  - Write "Female" / "Male": Micatro's pixel font (m6x11plus) has no female / male signs (U+2640 / U+2642), the reason upstream
    writes "Nidoran F".
  - The two branches can differ in shape: Wormadam keeps the Basic's state (the cloak), Mothim drops it for a deck-wide count
    (X(1 + 0.25 per DIFFERENT enhancement in the full deck), of the 8 vanilla enhancements + Pokermon's Seed and Flower; Hazard and
    the April Fools Ludicolo don't count; up to X3.5; `Xmult_mod` +20%: 0.3 / 0.35 / 0.4).

House pattern: a ROLLED-ONCE TRAIT with its own art (the owner's Shellos line's sea, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js SEAS; Tatsugiri's
nature and Burmy's gender are the same roll without the art):
  - Rolled in the center's set_ability(self, card, initial) when `initial` (a new card: shop, pack, add_joker, create_card): the
    run's seeded pseudorandom with the joker's own seed key ('shellos_sea'), so a seed always rolls the same trait. Never re-rolled:
    rounds, copies (copy_card keeps ability.extra) and evolution keep it.
  - Store it in ability.extra.form when it should survive evolution (systems/evolution.js NAMES_TO_KEEP always restores `form`);
    the view gets it as poke.form (systems/anim.js view_card). Save / resume rebuild the run from its recipe, so the same seed rolls
    the same trait again: nothing to persist by hand. Give the config a default (form: 'east'), the trait of the center's own cell.
  - Per-trait art: where upstream draws one cell per form (pokespriteload.lua lists two `base.pos` for Shellos / Gastrodon), the
    center keeps the FIRST cell and ui.js ownFace draws the other from a table (SEA_SPRITES), keyed by center and form, like
    Castform's CASTFORM_SPRITES. basicShiny then swaps in the Shiny sheet's twin at the same position, so Shiny works for free.
    Look at the cells to name them (Shellos {4,1} is blue = East, {6,1} pink = West), and pin it with a pixel test that cuts the
    cell each card is DRAWN from out of its sheet (pokermon_shellos_test 'art cells': West cells are pink). The Pokedex modal
    shows the family in the opened card's form (ui.js cardOf). The mods wiki (gen_designwiki_mods.mjs) shows one cell per joker.
  - A rolled SIDE (Shellos West = the leftmost scored card, East the rightmost) is a position rule: read the end of
    context.scoring_hand by position; a debuffed card there scores nothing (the end never moves inward). A 1-card hand's card is
    both ends, so a rule on both ends must apply once (test the card against each end, not count the ends).
  - Two Seas (a cross-trait unlock): holding both traits (any mix of the line, undebuffed; a Blueprint is not one) unlocks both
    sides for every copy. The tooltip says what a shop card WOULD do once bought (its own trait counts with the row's).

2.3 Evolution
-------------
Mechanisms (systems/evolution.js). Every evolution happens in the main end_of_round pass, never
from a Blueprint copy. An owned Everstone blocks all evolution, but rounds still count down.

  level_evo     extra.rounds counts down by 1 each round won (not while debuffed).
                Footer: "(Evolves after N rounds)". 113 jokers use it.
  scaling_evo   Evolves when a tracked value reaches config.evo_rqmt.
                Footers: "(Evolves at +X / +16 Mult)" (11), "... Chips" (8), "... X Mult" (5),
                "(Evolves after triggering N times)" (17). There are also bespoke counts:
                "after earning $16", "after playing 6 Full Houses", "after scoring 15 Gold
                cards", "after using 20 consumables", "after rerolling 7 times".
                House: "after 40 discarded cards" (Trapinch; Vibrava 60 more), counting each card a
                player discard holds, with a "(Discarded: k/40)" progress line.
                House: "after 8 Pink Seal triggers" (Spoink), with a "(Pink Seal triggers: k/8)" line.
                House: "after Spitting Up 4 times" (Drifloon: a final-hand release with 1+ Stockpile; an empty
                one doesn't count), with a "(Spit Ups: k/4)" line.
  item_evo      An evolution Item sets extra.evolve, and the joker evolves at the end of the next
                round. item_req names the item.
                  Linking Cord (all 10 trade evolutions)   Moon Stone (7)   Dusk Stone (5)
                  Thunder, Leaf, Water Stone (4 each)      Fire, Dawn Stone (3 each)
                  Shiny, Sun Stone (2 each)
                  King's Rock, Prism Scale, Upgrade, Dubious Disc, Hard Stone, Ice Stone
                Branches use evo_list. Gloom takes Leaf/Sun, Poliwhirl takes Water Stone/King's
                Rock, Charcadet takes Dawn/Dusk, Eevee takes eight stones.
  type_evo      Evolves while holding a Tera sticker of a type: Onix (Metal), Scyther (Metal
                sticker or Hard Stone), Seadra (Dragon), Pokermon+'s Duraludon (Metal).
  deck_*_evo    Evolves on deck composition: Chansey (25% Lucky), Nacho's Snover (12.5% Glass),
                Nacho's Dipplin (25% Wild).
  custom        Branching by play counters (Rockruff picks its Lycanroc by counting Even and Odd
                cards scored), "after 4 rounds not debuffed" (Volatile jokers), "4 rounds in
                debt" (Snorunt), "after defeating 2 Boss Blinds" (Shroomish).
                House: a GENDERED level_evo (the owner's Burmy: 4 rounds, Female -> Wormadam, Male -> Mothim;
                the gender rolled when the card is created, 2.1).

Typical round timers (level_evo):
  Baby     always 2 rounds (all 18)
  Basic    4 rounds (43) or 5 rounds (34); 3 rounds (4); 2 rounds (Caterpie, Weedle, Koffing);
           Magikarp 8
  Stage 1  4 (6), 5 (5), 3 (3); Shelgon 8 (it counts down faster on Straights)
  Rule of thumb: early-evolving Pokemon (around Lv 15-20) get 4 rounds, normal ones 5, and
  bug lines 2-3.

Typical scaling thresholds:
  +Mult   Basic evolves at +8 to +18 (Paras 8, Zubat 12, Horsea 12, Dratini 15, Sentret 15,
          Charmander 16, Shuppet 18). Stage 1 evolves around 2-3x higher (Charmeleon 36,
          Dragonair 55).
  Chips   Basic 36-60 (Swablu 36, Squirtle 40, Baltoy 40, Beldum 50, Ponyta 60, Slugma 60).
          Stage 1 about 3-4x (Wartortle 120, Metang 200).
  X Mult  Basic X1.5-X2 (Mareep 1.5, Aron 2, Pawniard 2); Stage 1 X2.5-X4 (Flaaffy 2.5,
          Lairon 4).

What carries over (copy_scaled_values): scaled values (mult, chips, Xmult, Xmult_multi and money
that have a matching _mod field), plus poke_custom_values_to_keep. Energy is re-applied to the new
base values. Edition, Tera sticker, eternal and rental status carry over; a Perishable's tally
resets. Design the line so that the inherited value matters (Charmander's Mult becomes
Charizard's flat Mult).

How much a Stage 1 or Stage 2 should out-scale its Basic, measured on lines that keep the same
field:
  Basic -> Stage 1    flat +Mult x1.67 (range 1-5)     Chips x1.5 (1-3)
                      X gain per trigger x1.5          Mult/Chip gain per trigger x1-1.67
                      (often unchanged; the stage adds a second effect instead)
                      Hand size, hands, discards and retriggers usually stay the same.
  Stage 1 -> Stage 2  flat +Mult x1.5 (0.5-2.3)        Chips x1.4
                      Mult gain per trigger about x1 (Stage 2 changes the kind instead)
Second ability box ("{br:2}" in the text):
  Basic 18% (47/267)   Stage 1 38% (84/219)   Stage 2 40% (27/68)   Legendary 38%
X Mult appears in:
  18% of Basics, 23% of Stage 1s, 43% of Stage 2s

The patterns behind those numbers:
  (a) Same effect, bigger number.
      Weedle (+20 Chips) -> Kakuna (+40) -> Beedrill (+80).
      Geodude (+75 Chips / -1 hand size) -> Graveler (+175 / -2) -> Golem (+275 / -3).
  (b) Same trigger, the payoff moves from + to X.
      Oddish: odd cards give +1/+3/+5 Mult -> Gloom +3/+5/+7 -> Vileplume +7, X1.3 or X1.5.
      Cyndaquil: +4 Mult per remaining discard -> Quilava +8 -> Typhlosion +8 and X0.3 each.
  (c) The Stage 1 or Stage 2 keeps the old effect and adds a second box.
      Grimer -> Muk; Houndour -> Houndoom; Electrike -> Manectric (Gold card returns to hand).
  (d) The final stage switches mechanic, often to a rule-bender.
      Gastly/Haunter (chance of a Negative) -> Gengar (a guaranteed Negative in 5 rounds).
      Abra -> Alakazam (+1 consumable slot).
      Slakoth -> Vigoroth -> Slaking (X4, -1 hand).

Baby Pokemon (rules.js faint_baby_poke):
  - Rule: "When hand is played, if there are any non-Baby Jokers to the right of this Joker,
    debuff this Joker" (it faints).
  - Every Baby has a Mult penalty: X0.75 usually; X0.5 for Tyrogue, Mime Jr., Happiny and
    Munchlax; X0.9 for Riolu. The field is Xmult_minus, which Energy does not touch.
  - In exchange each Baby has a utility. Pichu earns $10 at end of round. Cleffa, Igglybuff,
    Azurill, Wynaut, Chingling and Mantyke create a Negative tarot. Budew creates a Negative
    Miracle Seed. Smoochum and Elekid create a Tag when they evolve. Magby gives +1 discard.
  - Baby rarity is U $4, or C $3 for the money/tarot ones.

2.4 Energy and energize (systems/energy.js)
-------------------------------------------
How it works:
  - Using an Energy card of the joker's type (leftmost or selected joker) "energizes" it. Every
    numeric field listed in energy_values goes up by a share of the center's BASE value of that
    field.
  - Colorless Energy works on any typed joker at half strength.
  - Double Rainbow Energy (Spectral) energizes any type twice; Berry Juice Energy works on any
    type.
  - Limit: 3 Energy per joker (energy_max). G.GAME.energy_plus raises it: Porygon +1, Porygon2
    +2, Porygon-Z +3, Ludicolo +1, and Mega Mewtwo Y +1 per Boss Blind defeated.

Share of the BASE value added per Energy:
  mult, mult1, mult2, s_mult                  +40%
  chips, chips1-3                             +30%
  money, money1, money2                       +30%
  money_mod                                   +10%
  mult_mod, mult_mod2, chip_mod               +20%
  Xmult, Xmult1, Xmult2, Xmult_mod            +20%   (an X2 field becomes X2.4)
  Xmult_multi, Xmult_multi2                   +5%    (per-card X: X1.5 becomes X1.575)
  Xchips +20%, Xchips_multi +5%

Fields Energy never touches: rounds, retriggers, h_size, hands, d_size, card_limit, num/dem
(probabilities), Xmult_minus, hazard_level, scry; house: slot_cost (Purugly's -1 Joker slot).

Designer consequences:
  1. The field name is a balance choice. A joker-wide X stored as Xmult grows 20% of its base
     per Energy; a per-card X stored as Xmult_multi grows only 5% (on purpose, because it fires
     once per card).
  2. A pure retrigger, hand-size or Foresight joker gains nothing from Energy. That is fine
     (Rattata). If you want the joker to want Energy, give it a numeric rider (Victreebel's
     +24 Chips on the cards it retriggers).
  3. Scaling jokers whose accumulator starts at 0 (mult: 0) gain only through their _mod field.
  4. precise_energy (default ON) keeps fractional values.
  5. A BANKED total (house pattern: Whismur's glass_chips, Trapinch's sand_chips) lives in a field
     energize ignores, with its gain in an energy field (chip_mod / mult_mod / Xmult_mod). Energy
     then raises only future gains, never what is already banked (not retroactive), and the bank
     carries through evolution by poke_custom_values_to_keep.
  6. An OVERFLOW gain (house pattern: Grumpig's Black Pearl) is the normal gain doubled, computed
     from the same Xmult_mod (2 x Xmult_mod), so one energy field scales both and the ratio holds.
  7. ENERGY RAISING A COUNT (house: the owner's Regice, `glass_convert`): a count with no other number (cards converted per Blind)
     gets its own energy_values entry set to 1 (the whole base: +1 per Energy, +0.5 per mismatched Colorless, floored by the
     joker), the shape of Gulpin's `stockpile_max` (1/3 of a base 3). Only the joker carrying the field changes.
     A LATER STAGE WITH A BIGGER CAP keeps +1 per Energy by splitting the cap (house: the owner's Drifblim): the energy field
     stays at the line's base (stockpile_max 3, so a third is still exactly 1) and the extra is a fixed non-energy field
     (stockpile_bonus 2; the cap reads floor(stockpile_max) + stockpile_bonus = 5, 8 with 3 Energy). Writing the bigger base into
     the energy field instead would make each Energy worth a third of IT (5/3). Energy carried through evolution is re-applied
     to the new base, so Drifloon's 3 Energy (max 6) become Drifblim's max 8.

Energy sources:
  Energy packs; the Pink Seal; Porygon (on any Booster opened); Porygon2 (of the leftmost
  joker's type); Porygon-Z (when you use an Energy); Pyroar (Flush with a King or Queen);
  Ludicolo (2-card hands); Jelly Donut; Nightmare (Spectral).

House patterns around the Pink Seal (the owner's Spoink line, jokers/pokejokers_designs.js):
  - Seal-adding joker: at end of round a random held card WITH NO SEAL gains a Pink Seal (Pearl:
    the run's seeded pseudorandom_element; none unsealed, nothing; not Blueprint, once a round).
  - "Trigger every hand" override: Pink Seals fire only in the round's first hand (upstream), but
    seals1.js's check is `hands_played === 0 || find_card('j_poke_grumpig').length > 0`: a narrow
    key check, so with no (undebuffed) Grumpig nothing changes for vanilla, other mods or the
    Obituary Deck. Each trigger still runs the Obituary fall-off roll and the Bird Energy removal.
  - Counting triggers: the seal calls every owned joker's center hook poke_pink_trigger(self,
    joker, seal_card, overflow) once per trigger (retriggers count again), with or without room
    for the Energy. A Blueprint has no hook, so it copies the X Mult and never counts.
  - Overflow-to-X-Mult: when the seal has no consumable room (overflow), Grumpig gains X0.3
    instead of X0.15 for that hand: once per hand, the overflow gain REPLACING the normal one
    (a hand with 3 seals, one overflowed, gains X0.3 total). G.GAME.hands_played names the hand.

Energy-themed jokers:
  Ralts/Kirlia: +Mult per Energized Joker.
  Gardevoir: X0.3 per Energized Joker.
  Gallade: X0.5 per Joker with 2+ Energy.
  Porygon-Z: X0.1 per Energy used.
  Gastrodon (the owner's Storm Drain): banks X0.1 per Energy card used, X0.2 for Water Energy.

House pattern: ENERGY USED ANYWHERE (the owner's Gastrodon, Storm Drain; Porygon-Z's trigger):
  - context.using_consumeable with consumeable.ability.set === 'Energy': the 12 type Energies and Bird Energy, on ANY joker (or
    energizing nothing: a mismatched Colorless still counts). Double Rainbow Energy (a Spectral) and Berry Juice Energy (an Item)
    are not in the Energy set and don't count; say so if a design means them.
  - A matching type pays more (Water Energy: consumeable.config.center.key === 'c_poke_water_energy'), derived from the same gain
    field (2 x Xmult_mod), so one energy field scales both and the ratio holds.
  - Order: the Energy's use() runs first, so an Energy that energizes the joker itself is counted at the NEW gain (a Water Energy
    on a fresh Gastrodon banks 2 x 0.12). Banked in a non-energy field (drain_Xmult): not retroactive. Not Blueprint (it scales).

2.6 Hazards, Seeds and Flowers, Wishes, Unown
---------------------------------------------
Hazards (3.9.1 Hazard layer; enhancements/enhancement1.js and systems/utils.js)
  - Hazard card: "no rank or suit", no chips, always scores. It has a 1 in 3 chance to be
    destroyed if held in hand at end of round. It stays in the deck until destroyed and takes a
    hand slot (discard it to draw).
  - Hazard layer: when the first hand of a round is drawn, that many Hazard cards are dealt into
    the hand. The layer is capped by the hazard limit (default 3).
  - Jokers write "{C:poke_hazard}+1{} hazard layer" and spread hazard_layer (add/remove), which
    raises G.GAME.poke_hazard_level. Skarmory and Mega Skarmory raise the limit too ("hazard
    layer and limit").
  - Payoffs:
      Roggenrola line   cards with no rank held give +5/+10/+7 Mult (+ retrigger)
      Skarmory          X0.4 per Hazard or Steel card held
      Golett/Golurk     held cards give X1.2/X1.3 by chance, guaranteed for Hazard cards
      Baltoy/Claydol    chips per Hazard card discarded
      Cacnea/Cacturne   $3/$5 when a card is destroyed
      Ferroseed line    Hazard and Wild cards are also Steel
      Nacho's Carbink   Hazards also count as Gold
  - Counter: Heavy-Duty Boots (reusable Item: Hazard probabilities become 0 for the round).
  - 19 non-Mega jokers mention Hazards, spread over Earth (5), Grass (6), Psychic, Metal, Dark,
    Water and Fairy.

Seeds and Flowers (3.9.1)
  - Seed card: "Grows 1 (+growth level) when scored". After 5 growths it turns into a Flower
    card and earns $15. Seed is in the Enhanced pool (weight 5). Miracle Seed (Item) makes one.
  - Flower card: X2 Mult if the poker hand contains 4+ suits. With Roserade it needs only 3+
    suits; with Shaymin or Shaymin Sky it gives X3.
  - Growth level (+1 extra growth per score): Shiftry, Brambleghast.
  - Seed makers: the Hoppip line (discards become Seeds, then the joker self-destructs on
    discard), Bellossom, Roselia, Nuzleaf/Shiftry (drawn Flowers become Seeds), Bramblin
    (second Queen scored each round), Budew (a Negative Miracle Seed).

Wishes (Jirachi)
  - Jirachi (Legendary): "At the end of the shop, make a wish!" This opens a Wish Pack of 3
    random unused wishes, and Jirachi transforms into the chosen form:
      Banker      3X payout at end of round
      Booster     +1 card in packs, and the first 3 rerolls add a Booster
      Power       played cards give X2 every 3 hands
      Invisible   copies the joker to the right if you have 3+ Energized jokers
      Fixer       a single-card first discard becomes Death, Cryptid or Metal Coat
      Copy        copies the joker to the right with +1 Energy
      Negging     +2 Joker slots, Negative 2X more often
  - The Wish template is a good one for any "choose your form" legendary.

Unown
  - Unown (Safari Basic $5): "Nature: (rank) / +6 Mult if poker hand contains a Nature card",
    and it self-destructs at end of round.
  - Ruins of Alph (Other, R $8) creates 3 Negative Unown each Blind, merges an Unown after it
    triggers (+2 Mult each), and pays out by Unown merged (5 / 10 / 20 / 28). At 28 the reward
    is Unown Swarm, where each Legendary Joker gives +28 Mult and X2.8.

2.7 Held items ("Holding")
--------------------------
The "Holding X" tooltip reads: "This Joker comes with a X card (Must have room)". The joker
creates its signature consumable in add_to_deck. Not when re-added from a debuff; sometimes as
a Negative so it always fits. The ability then refers to the item.

Pokermon's held items:
  Farfetch'd          Leek: $4 is guaranteed when the Leek is used
  Cubone              Thick Club: counts double toward its Mult
  Snorlax             Leftovers: gains X0.2 per Leftovers you have
  Hypno               Trance (Spectral)
  Ninetales           a Negative Medium
  Mega Victreebel     Medium
  Mega Feraligatr     Dragon Scale
  Gardevoir           Black Hole
  Spinda              Wheel of Fortune
  Luvdisc             Heart Scale
  Gallade             Double Rainbow Energy
  Farigiraf           Cryptid
Add-ons: Nacho's Terapagos (a Negative Tera Orb), Pokermon+'s Darkrai (Nightmare), Pokermon+'s
Meltan (a Negative Metal Coat).

Three uses:
  (a) The item is the joker's engine: Snorlax, Cubone, Farfetch'd.
  (b) A thematic vanilla consumable that comes "for free": Hypno's Trance, Gardevoir's Black
      Hole, Spinda's Wheel of Fortune, Farigiraf's Cryptid.
  (c) A rare resource: Gallade's Double Rainbow Energy.
Pokermon's reusable cards (marked "Usable once per round"): Leftovers, Leek, Thick Club,
Heavy-Duty Boots, the Mega Stone, the Meteorite. Evolution Items double as minor effects: the
Moon Stone has a chance to level up the selected poker hand, and the Fire Stone makes Mult
cards.

2.8 Megas (systems/items.js)
----------------------------
How Megas work:
  - The Mega Stone is a reusable Spectral, usable once per round. It Mega Evolves a joker that
    has megas[], or devolves a Mega back. Only 1 joker can Mega Evolve per Mega Stone you have.
    Selling the stone devolves its Mega.
  - The Mega Berry Juice (an Item) Mega Evolves the leftmost or selected joker.
  - Charizard, Mewtwo and Raichu have two Megas: leftmost gives X, rightmost gives Y, anywhere
    else is random (getMega_by_position).
  - There are 41 Megas: 34 Pokermon, 6 Nacho, 1 Pokermon+. Rarity poke_mega, cost $12 is
    standard, and none of them evolve.

Mega design patterns:
  1. One enormous stat.
     Mega Beedrill +640 Chips; Mega Venusaur +4 hand size; Mega Blastoise +3 hands;
     Mega Charizard Y +5 discards.
  2. The base condition pushed to its extreme.
     Mega Charizard X: X5 at 0 discards. Mega Slowbro: X0.5 per hand played this Ante.
     Mega Starmie: Diamonds give X2.5 and $5 on 2-card hands.
  3. A rule-bender.
     Mega Gyarados: X3 and disables every Boss Blind. Mega Kangaskhan: retrigger all played
     cards. Mega Mewtwo X: all Jokers give X2.5 ($30). Mega Ampharos: hand size becomes half the
     deck. Mega Drampa: packs are always Mega.
  4. A power spike with a drawback.
     Mega Heracross retriggers all played cards twice but debuffs itself unless 5 cards are
     played or discarded. Mega Scizor makes Commons give X4, then destroys them. Mega
     Aerodactyl has a 1 in 4 chance to destroy each Ace.
Rule of thumb: a Mega trades the line's flexibility for one dramatic axis, and it is temporary
by nature (you can devolve it once per round).

2.9 Shiny (systems/shiny.js)
----------------------------
  - The Shiny edition (e_poke_shiny) has shop weight 2 and costs +$5.
  - It has no scoring effect. While a shiny card is owned, the shop has +1 Booster Pack slot.
  - Playing cards can't become shiny unless the shiny_playing_cards option is on.
  - Design rule: never put power in "if this is shiny". Shiny is an economy and cosmetic layer.
    (Nacho's Mega Audino Mystery Egg rolling a 1 in 5 shiny is the only joker that touches it.)

2.10 Text style and the poke_ colours
-------------------------------------
Pokermon's text is short: 4-6 lines of about 25-32 characters. Its conventions:
  - Numbers carry their colour: {C:mult}+4{} Mult, {C:chips}+30{} Chips,
    {X:mult,C:white} X1.5 {} Mult (note the spaces inside), {C:money}$3{}.
  - Keywords use {C:attention}: Blind, poker hand names, ranks, enhancements.
  - The second ability box is a "{br:2}ERROR - CONTACT STEAK" line (the engine draws a divider).
  - The state line: "{C:inactive}(Currently {C:mult}+0{C:inactive} Mult)".
  - The evolution footer is small grey:
      "{C:inactive,s:0.8}(Evolves after {C:attention,s:0.8}#2#{C:inactive,s:0.8} rounds)"
  - Odds: "{C:green}#1# in #2#{} chance". Always read them through get_probability_vars so
    Venonat, Absol and others can modify them.
  - "{C:inactive,s:0.8}(Must have room)" for anything that creates a card.
  - "{S:1.1,C:red,E:2}self destructs{}" for self-destruction.
Type and keyword colours (pokesprites.lua pokermon.colours; Micatro TYPE_COLOURS):
  {C:poke_<type>} or {X:poke_<type>,C:white} for a type name. Lightning is
    {X:poke_lightning,C:black}: black on yellow.
    Hex values:
      grass 289830    fire f81020      water 38b8f8      lightning f8f800
      psychic c135ff  fighting b85838  colorless c8c0f8  dark 0086a5
      metal 888080    fairy ff3db6     dragon c8a800     earth e97333
  {C:poke_pink}    Energy / Energized (30 uses)
  {C:poke_item}    Item cards (35 uses)
  {C:poke_hazard}  hazard layer (15 uses)
  {C:purple}       Foresight (vanilla purple)
  {C:poke_bird}    Missingno
  {C:poke_pocket}  Pocket Packs
  poke_sun, poke_rain, poke_sand, poke_snow: defined in 3.9.1 for weather designs (Castform,
    Kyogre/Groudon, Hippowdon). All four are now used (Kyogre / Groudon too: "always {C:poke_rain}Rainy{}"): poke_sand by the owner's Flygon
    ("{C:poke_sand}Sandstorm:"), poke_sun / poke_rain / poke_snow by the owner's Castform
    ("{C:poke_sun}Sunny Form:", "{C:poke_rain}Rainy Form:", "{C:poke_snow}Snowy Form:").
Most used tags overall:
  attention 1166, inactive 540, mult 230, X:mult 218, chips 184, money 122, br:2 106,
  green 77, dark_edition 49.

==================================================================================================
4. GENERAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES
==================================================================================================

4.1 Evolution lines that feel like growth
-----------------------------------------
  - Keep the trigger and grow the payoff. Players learn the Basic's condition, and the Stage 1
    and Stage 2 reward them for keeping it. That is the Oddish, Cyndaquil, Squirtle and Dratini
    pattern.
  - Make the value carry over. If the Basic scales (mult plus mult_mod, chips plus chip_mod,
    Xmult plus Xmult_mod), the evolution inherits it, so a well-fed Basic is a better
    Stage 1.
  - Step sizes, from section 2.3:
      Basic to Stage 1    about x1.5-1.7 on the main number
      Stage 1 to Stage 2  about x1.4-1.5, or swap + for X
      About 40% of Stage 1s and Stage 2s add a second ability box, against 18% of Basics
  - Give each stage a clear job:
      Basic    simple and on-curve, with a 4-5 round timer or a reachable threshold
      Stage 1  the main engine
      Stage 2  either a big flat number (Golem, Dragonite) or a rule change (Gengar, Slaking,
               Kingambit)
  - Pick the evolution kind from the real game:
      trade evolutions        Linking Cord
      stone evolutions        the matching stone
      friendship or time      rounds
      level evolutions        rounds, or a play-count threshold that teaches the mechanic
                              ("after triggering N times")
      branching evolutions    evo_list items, or Rockruff-style play counters
  - Pre-evolutions that are deliberately weak (Magikarp, Feebas) are fine when the payoff is
    dramatic. Price them low (C $2, R $3).

4.2 Flavour tied to the Pokemon
-------------------------------
The best existing jokers read their signature move, ability or Pokedex entry literally:
  Rollout-like growth; Slaking's Truant (-1 hand); Absol's disaster sense (probabilities 0);
  Wobbuffet's Shadow Tag (Eternal); Delibird's Present; Ditto's Transform; Smeargle's Sketch;
  Snorunt and Glalie's "debt" ice; Zangoose adding cards (Crush Claw); Mimikyu's Disguise
  (prevents death); Gholdengo's "can't be debuffed" (Good as Gold).
A good test: someone who knows the Pokemon should guess roughly what the card does from its name.
Prefer, in this order:
  1. the signature move or ability
  2. the Pokedex fact
  3. the type identity
Mechanic keywords the community already knows (Volatile, Nature, Ancient, Foresight, Hazards,
Holding, Drain) should be reused before new ones are invented.

4.3 Avoiding copies
-------------------
Before finalising a design, check it against three lists:
  - The same type's existing shapes (section 3). For example, Fire already rewards 0 discards
    four times.
  - The vanilla jokers listed in the "close to vanilla" notes. A Pokermon joker that only
    re-skins a vanilla joker needs a twist: a type, an evolution, a second box. Even existing
    ones do this: Krabby is Scary Face at 2/3 strength, but it grows into Kingler's Bonus-card
    conversion.
  - The hooks. If your effect is "on joker_main, +N if hand contains X", there are ~40 of those
    already. Reach for the less-used hooks:
      hand_drawn (32), using_consumeable (25), remove_playing_cards (23), open_booster (14),
      ending_shop (14), selling_card (7), reroll_shop (6), playing_card_added (6),
      skipping_booster (4)
    The Steamodded seams from batch 4 (debuff_card, modify_scoring_hand, drawing_cards,
    money_altered, modify_final_cashout, stay_flipped, modify_hand, poker_hand_changed) each
    have only 1-2 Pokermon users so far.

4.4 Balance by rarity
---------------------
  Common Basic ($4-$5)
    One condition, small number: +3 to +10 Mult, +40 to +75 Chips, $1-$3, or X1.5-X1.75 with a
    real condition (Voltorb's Volatile). It evolves quickly.
  Uncommon Basic ($5-$6, the 3-stage default)
    The first piece of an engine. Scaling steps of +1 to +2 Mult, +4 to +12 Chips or X0.1-0.25,
    or a utility (+1 hand size, +1 discard, Foresight).
  Rare Basic ($7-$9, the single-stage default)
    Has to stand alone forever. X2 with a condition (Pinsir, Heracross, Absol), a rule change
    (Mr. Mime, Lucario), or an engine (Electabuzz).
  Safari Stage 1 ($7-$9)
    The main engine. X2-X2.5 flat is the ceiling (Gyarados X2.5 after 8 rounds of Magikarp).
  Safari Stage 2 ($10)
    The full engine plus one more thing. X3-X4 needs a cost or condition (Slaking -1 hand,
    Hydreigon destroying cards).
  Legendary ($20)
    A unique rule (Moltres, Celebi, Mew, Ho-oh) or a high-ceiling scaler (Zapdos, Lugia). Never
    just big numbers.
  Mega ($12)
    One axis, pushed to the extreme, often with a drawback (2.8).
  Baby ($3-$4)
    Penalty X0.75 (or X0.5 for strong utilities) plus one utility, and always 2 rounds.
Probability effects should go through pseudorandom_probability/get_probability_vars, so Venonat
(+1 numerator), Togekiss, Leafeon (x3), Nacho's Victini (x3) and Absol (always 0) affect them.
Price for that:
  - "1 in 2" becomes guaranteed with one Venonat.
  - A "chance to fail" becomes a chance to succeed under Absol.

4.5 How Megas, shinies and held items layer on
----------------------------------------------
  - Megas: design one only for species that have a canonical Mega (or Gigantamax-style
    showpieces if the owner wants). The Mega should be the line's mechanic at 3-5x, or a rule
    break, at $12. It must stay interesting with the once-per-round devolve: Megas are switched
    in for the Boss Blind.
  - Shiny: never touches ability text. Assume any joker can be Shiny (+$5 cost, +1 booster
    slot).
  - Held items: add one when the Pokemon has a canonical held item or signature item (Leek,
    Thick Club, Leftovers, Lucky Egg/Punch, Quick Powder, Metal Powder, Dragon Scale, Deep Sea
    Tooth or Scale, Razor Claw or Fang, Rusted Sword or Shield...). Let the ability reference
    it, and make it "(Must have room)" or Negative.
  - Energy: decide which numbers should scale. Only fields in energy_values do (2.4). A
    retrigger or hand-size joker that should want Energy needs a numeric rider.
