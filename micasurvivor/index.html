MicaSurvivor is a free, non-commercial fan game on Magepunk, based on PokéSurvivor by JPTER78. No ads, no purchases — everything is earned by playing.

Privacy

Playing as a guest, MicaSurvivor saves your progress only in this browser and makes no network requests. Logged in to Magepunk, your save and your best survival time are stored with your Magepunk account (and a copy stays in this browser).
