The Basics

A Hearthstone-style card battler with a Magic: The Gathering heart — lands, instants, the stack, planes, and up to 8 players around one table.

40Starting Life
40Cards per Deck
2–8Players
12Max Mana Crystals

Every player is a hero with 40 Life, a class with a hero power, and a 40-card deck. With 3 or more players it's a free-for-all around a circular table — each player gets their own slice of the board, turns pass clockwise, and the last hero standing wins. There is no healing cap: your Life can climb above 40.

Setup

  • Opening hands: the first player draws 3 cards; everyone else draws 4 cards plus The Coin (a 0-cost card: “Gain 1 mana this turn only”). There is no mulligan — you keep what you're dealt.
  • Companion & Commander: when building your deck you may optionally include a Companion and a Commander. They don't sit in your deck — they start the game in their own zones, playable from there; a Commander that dies returns to the command zone and costs 2 more each time.
  • Turn one: the first player takes the normal start-of-turn draw, so they effectively see 4 cards on their first turn too.

Deck rules

  • A standard deck is 40 cards, from your class's cards plus neutrals (multi-class cards count for each listed class). An optional Companion and Commander come on top of the 40, so a full deck can reach 42 cards.
  • At most 2 copies of any card, and only 1 copy of each Legendary.
  • Lands, colored W/U/B/R/G cards, and tokens are never deck cards — you get those during play.

Build decks in the game's deck builder, and browse every card in the Card Gallery.

Your Turn

No rigid phases — on your turn you act in any order you like.

  • Mana: you start the game with 1 mana crystal, and your maximum grows by 1 at the start of each of your turns (up to 12). Unspent mana never carries over — whatever you don't use is gone at end of turn, and your crystals refill to your new maximum when your next turn starts. Temporary mana (The Coin, land taps, “gain mana” effects) also vanishes at end of turn — and unlike crystals, it doesn't come back. Cards with Overload lock that much of your mana next turn.
  • Draw: you draw 1 card at the start of every turn.
  • Do anything, in any order: play cards, attack, use each hero power once, buy or tap lands, use each creature's activated ability once, planeswalk, cash in tokens, trade Tradeable cards… then press End Turn.
  • Hand limit: 15 cards. You never burn a draw — you can go over during your turn, then discard down to 15 at end of turn (you choose what to keep).
  • Running out of cards: when your deck empties, your graveyard is shuffled back in (tokens excluded). Only when deck and graveyard are both empty does fatigue begin: each empty draw deals escalating damage to your hero (1, then 2, then 3…).
Zone limits
Your creature row is unlimited. Capped zones: 5 lands, 5 secrets, 3 traps, 3 quests, 3 hero-power slots.

Combat

Creatures trade blows simultaneously — and every attack can be responded to.

  • Declaring an attack goes on the stack — opponents can respond with instants before the damage lands.
  • Who you can hit: any opponent's creatures, planeswalkers, or hero. If a defender has Taunt creatures, your attacks must hit a Taunt first (each player's Taunts protect only their own board). Piercing ignores Taunt entirely. Stealthed and dormant creatures can't be attacked.
  • Attacks per turn: one per creature (Windfury: two). Creatures with 0 Attack, Pacifist, or that are Frozen or dormant can't attack.
  • Damage is simultaneous: attacker and defender deal each other their Attack — unless one has First Strike, which deals its damage first; if that kills the other creature, there's no strike back. Two First Strikes cancel out.
  • Hero attacks: your hero swings with weapon Attack + temporary hero Attack, once per turn (twice with a Windfury weapon). Attacking a creature means it strikes back at your hero. Each swing costs 1 weapon Durability. A temporary Attack buff lets even a weaponless hero swing.
  • Armor absorbs hero damage before Life (Piercing skips it too).

Combat is where most keywords live — Divine Shield, Lifesteal, Deathtouch, Cleave, Trample, Sanguine and more. See the full glossary below.

Lands & Colors

Lands aren't in your deck — you buy them mid-game, and they power a whole second color system.

  • On your turn, pay 3 mana to develop a land into one of your 5 land slots — and every opponent receives The Coin as compensation.
  • The five basics — Plains (W), Island (U), Swamp (B), Mountain (R), Forest (G) — set your color identity. Advanced lands unlock only when your basics cover their colors.
  • Tapping a basic, choose one: gain 1 mana, give a friendly creature that color's Boost, or Conjure a random card of that color into your hand.
  • Lands and locations double-tap: after tapping they stay tapped through your next turn, untapping the turn after — so each is usable roughly every other turn.
  • Any land can be sacrificed to draw a card.

Planes & the Planar Die

The whole table shares one arena plane — and daring mages can roll to change it.

  • The active plane belongs to nobody. It has an Arrival effect (fires when it appears), a Chaos ability, a Departure effect (fires when it leaves), and a continuous rule that applies the whole time it's the active plane.
  • Planeswalking requires a planeswalker in play or having used a Spark card.
  • Roll a d6: on a 6, Planeshift — swap to a random new plane (old Departure fires, new Arrival fires). On a 5, trigger the current plane's Chaos ability. 1–4: nothing.
  • Cost: your first roll each turn is free; each further roll that turn costs 1 more mana than the last (0, 1, 2…).
Example plane rules
Stronghold Furnace doubles all combat damage · Alkabah makes 4-cost spells free · Belenon stops things untapping. Browse every plane in the Card Gallery.

The Stack & Instants

Nothing resolves instantly — everything can be answered.

  • Spells, hero powers, activated abilities, land taps, and even attacks go on a last-in-first-out stack.
  • Opponents holding an instant (or an instant-speed ability or land tap) get a priority window to respond before the top of the stack resolves. Priority passes clockwise; when everyone passes, the top resolves.
  • Counter instants remove a spell from the stack entirely — it never resolves.
  • The game has an Auto-pass toggle: turn it off and you get a short window to respond at instant speed to everything.

Tokens are exiled when they die — they never enter the graveyard or reshuffle. (The Coin is a real card, not a token, and does reshuffle.)

Corpses

Every friendly creature death banks a Corpse for its owner. All classes accrue them, but only Death Knights see a counter — their cards spend Corpses for effects.

The Coin economy

The Coin (+1 temporary mana) flows constantly: players going second start with one, and every time anyone develops a land, all their opponents get one.

Winning & Losing

When a hero's Life reaches 0 they are eliminated — their whole board and zones are cleared and their slice of the table goes dark. The game ends when one player remains: last hero standing wins. If everyone dies simultaneously, it's a draw.

Game Modes & Getting Cards

Battle (vs AI)

2–8 player free-for-all against AI opponents. Rewards: win +100 gold, draw +50, loss +25.

Duels

Draft a deck from scratch and run it until you win big or bust out. Pick one of 11 heroes, choose a hero power from your class's options, then draft 10 cards — three choices at a time, from your class's cards plus neutrals.

  • The run ends at 12 wins or 3 losses. Clearing pays +1000 gold, the biggest purse in the game.
  • Loot after every game — win or lose. You're offered 3 themed buckets (Dragons, Murlocs, Big Bruisers, Lowbies, Deathrattles, Taunt Up… 17 in all); take one and all 3 of its cards join your deck.
  • After games 1, 5 and 9 you also pick a Passive (1 of 3, from over 100), and after games 3, 7 and 11 a Treasure card (1 of 3). A full clear ends with a 52-card monster deck.
  • No health scaling — both heroes start at the usual 40 Life. Instead your opponent is generated at exactly your power level: a random rival who has drafted 10 cards and taken the same number of buckets, passives and treasures you have.
  • Conceding ends the run and pays nothing.

Dungeon Run

The original run mode. Pick a class, take its fixed 10-card starter deck, and climb an 8-level boss ladder. In a run, you and the boss share a scaling life total — 15 HP at level 1, +5 per level. After each win, choose 1 of 3 themed card buckets (all 3 cards join your deck); after every odd level, also pick 1 of 3 Treasures — permanent run passives like double your starting Health or your spells cost (1) less. Clearing level 8 pays +500 gold.

Live PvP & spectating

Challenge a friend to a real-time match, or spectate a friend's board as they play, through the test realm account system.

Packs & collection

New players start with 300 gold and a starter collection (2× every common, 1× every uncommon). Packs cost 100 gold and hold 5 cards — the first four roll common 60% / uncommon 25% / rare 10% / epic 4% / legendary 1%, and the fifth is guaranteed rare or better (rare 75% / epic 20% / legendary 5%). Browse what you own in the collection viewer and build decks in the deck builder.
